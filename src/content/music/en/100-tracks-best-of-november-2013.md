Here are 100 tracks that I listened to and enjoyed in November 2013. I was into a lot of mellow, acoustic guitar music this month--lots of John Fahey and the Derek Trucks Band--things like that. I remember really enjoying Beth Orton's _Sugaring Season_ and Mason Jennings' _Always Been_ records, listening to a lot of Richard Hawley, Josh Rouse, Jolie Holland, McCarthy Trenching, Cate le Bon, Spoon, Charlotte Gainbourg, and Mavis Staples this month.
