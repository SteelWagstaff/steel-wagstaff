Here are 100 songs I listened to and liked in February 2014. A little less variety than in previous months, perhaps. Representing a few groups/albums pretty heavily. I was most excited about Beck's new _Morning Phase_ album, and also listened to a lot of Willy Mason, Paul Simon, Ana Tijoux, Luke Temple, Goldfrapp, J Roddy Walston and the Business, Mitote (a <a href="https:::::::::::://theaustintimes.com/2010/05/meet-mitote-playing-mexican-folk-music-with-chicano-sensibility/">group of friends</a> which featurehttps:s:s:s:s:s:s:s:s:s:s:s://www.aechavez.com/">Alex E. Chávez</a>, an ethnomusicologist/anthropologist whose job talk I filmed a year or so ago, and really enjoyed). Volcano Choir's _Repave _album, and The Mountain Goats' _All Hail West Texas_. Enjoy!
