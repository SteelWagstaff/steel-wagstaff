Here's a playlist of 100 songs I listened to and liked from the month of September. Some favorites from the month were Sarah Jaffe, The War on Drugs, Flume, Shad, Larry Gus's _rs Not Living_, Eef Barzelay's new _Gi_Come First_ EP, Snorri Helgason's _I'm G_ to Put My Name On Your Door_,  Tamikrest's _Chatma</_ Hammock's _Oblivion Hymns_, Bellows' _Blue Breath_ record,  Josephine Foster's _I'm a Dreamer_ album, and some classic Don McLean (I listened to this with Cedar and thought of my dad, who introduced me to Don McLean).

Enjoy!
