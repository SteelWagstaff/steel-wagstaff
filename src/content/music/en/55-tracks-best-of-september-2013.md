In September I remember listening to a few artists and albums pretty heavily: Ghostpoet's _Peanut Butter Blues and Melancholy Jam_, Frazey Ford's _Obadiah_, Eleanor Friedberger's _Personal Record_, Baths' _Obsidian_, Nathaniel Rateliff's _Falling Faster Than You Can Run_, Mika's _The Origin of Love_, Efterklang's _Magic Chairs_, The Duckworth Lewis Method's cricket concept album _Sticky Wickets_, J Roddy Walston and the Business's _Essential Tremors_, Fruit Bats' _Tripper_, and the Richard Swift Collection. Not much new stuff outside of those records, but there was a lot of sound that I enjoyed from those records, for sure.
