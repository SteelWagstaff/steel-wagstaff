being democratic has got to be absolutely non-dogmatic, a-political, unsystematic: whereas system, dogmatism and all the rest is found tolerable in Yeats Pound Eliot. … Have to write one’s perceptions, not argue one’s beliefs. And be overwhelmingly happy if they turn out not to be altogether unconscionable.                    — George Oppen, letter to his sister June, February 9, 1959.
