Who owns what in these enclosures? Whose
the mountain besieged to the very top,
the patient walls, yellow wheat, almond trees?
Would this fine domain be yours, yours
the house, the precious pond,
the child who cries on the lawn?
Ah, who can keep in his hands
the crumbling walls, the immutable flower,
dismantled estates and dried up wells?
Who will read dead families’ names
on the moss-grown forgotten graves?
And wind, and rocks, and death, whose are they?

-- translated by Paulène Aspel
