> <p>After great pain a formal feeling comes&ndash;<br/> The nerves sit ceremonious like tombs;<br/> The stiff Heart questions&ndash;was it He that bore?<br/> And yesterday&ndash;or centuries before?<br/> The feet, mechanical, go round<br/> A wooden way<br/> Of ground, or air, or ought,<br/> Regardless grown,<br/> A quartz contentment, like a stone.</p> <p>This is the hour of lead<br/> Remembered if outlived,<br/> As freezing persons recollect the snow&ndash;<br/> First chill, then stupor, then the letting go.</p>

— Emily Dickinson
