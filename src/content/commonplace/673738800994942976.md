> “Recalling the Past at T’Ung Pass” As if gathered together, the peaks of the ranges.As if raging, the waves on these banks.Winding along these mountains and riversthe road to the T’ung Pass.I look west & hesitant I lamenthere where opposing armies passed through.Palaces of countless rulers now but dust.Empires rise: people suffer.Empires fall: people suffer.– Chang Yang-Hao; ‘translation’ by “C. H. Knock” and “G. G. Gach”
