As if gathered together,  
the peaks of the ranges.  
As if raging,  
the waves on these banks.  
Winding along  
these mountains and rivers  
the road to the T’ung Pass.  
I look west  
& hesitant I lament  
here where  
opposing armies passed through.  
Palaces  
of countless rulers  
now but dust.  
Empires rise:  
people suffer.  
Empires fall:  
people suffer.

– Chang Yang-Hao; ‘translation’ by “C. H. Knock” and “G. G. Gach”
