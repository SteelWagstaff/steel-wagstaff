> There seem to be at least two kinds of poets I admire; those whom I know are good, have read and admired but do not usually choose to re-read, like say Milton; in contrast to this rather large group there is a small group of poets whose work I often re-read simply for pleasure because they seem to me to speak the truth. Most of these, like Marvell, are from the distant past, but some, like George are contemporary. I find it impossible to imagine not having George&rsquo;s work and I do hope he realises how important it is to so very many people.

— Roger Guedella writing to Mary Oppen in 1984, after learning that George Oppen had been moved into a nursing home.
