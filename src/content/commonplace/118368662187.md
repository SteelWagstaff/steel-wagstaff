> It was easy shooting, and I killed many bats, never enjoying it after the first one I killed. It was too easy. I was simply becoming a destroyer &hellip; and when a lost bat flew wildly into the downstairs one hot summer night, scaring us half to death &hellip; I waited until the bat lost its friskiness and landed on an upper beam of the living room and shot it. The bat dropped like a playing card. I always felt smaller than the small animals shot.

— <a href="https:::::::::://www.longhousepoetry.com/bobarnold.html">Bob Arnold</a>, <i>This Romance</i>
