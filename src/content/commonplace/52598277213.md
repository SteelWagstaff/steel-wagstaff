Claus Narr (d.1515), the court jester, in reply to the Elector of Saxony <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Frederick_I,_Elector_of_Saxony">Johann Friedrich I</a>, who was lamenting that he had “lost the day”: _Morgen wollen wir alle fleissig suchcn, und den Tag, den du verloren hast, wohl wieder finden._ (Tomorrow we will all diligently seek for the day you have lost, and no doubt we shall find it again).

— cited in <a href="https://www.answers.com/topic/wolfgang-b-tner">Wolfgang Bütner</a> in: _627 Historien von Claus Narren_, 21, 51 (1572).
