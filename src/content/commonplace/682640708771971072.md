“I was in Paris when I heard about the assassination of four students of the School of Youth for Social Service, a school I had helped start. I cried. A friend named Mr. Windmiller said, "Thầy, you should not cry. You are a general leading an army of nonviolent soldiers. It is natural that you suffer casualties.” I said, “No, I am not a general. I am just a human being. It is I who summoned them for service, and now they have lost their lives. I need to cry.”  
– from the gloss to “Let Me Give Back to Our Motherland” by Thich Nhat Hanh. Included in *Call Me By My True Names*
