I once fell down in the Cut  
from hunger & I was  
lifted into Watchorn’s he said  
give the poor fellow a little drop of brandy  
& after that a biscuit the   
best things he can have he  
saved my life sir the people at the bar  
they see’d it was no humbug  
gathered sevenpence ha’penny for me  
a penny a-piece & a poor woman  
as I was going away  
slipt a couple of trotters into my hand
