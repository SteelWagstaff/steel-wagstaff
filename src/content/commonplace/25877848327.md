> Dear Pound,<br/> You&rsquo;ll see that the back cover of this book [The Materials] quotes an introduction you wrote for my poems many years ago. I was in fact about twenty-one.<br/> I suppose if we should take to talking politics to each other I would disagree even more actively than all those others who have disagreed, but there has been no one living during my life time who has been as generous or as pure as you toward literature and toward writers. Nor anyone less generously thanked. <br/> I know of no one who does not owe you a debt.<br/> George Oppen

— Oppen&rsquo;s letter to Ezra Pound in September or October 1962, upon the publication of _The Materials_, his first book of poetry in almost 30 years.
