  <blockquote><p><b>César Vallejo</b> (born 16 March, 1892; died 15 April, 1938), pictured above in Berlin 1929.</p>
  <p><b>Un hombre pasa con un pan al hombro</b></p>
  <p>Un hombre pasa con un pan al hombro<br>¿Voy a escribir, después, sobre mi doble?<br><br>Otro se sienta, ráscase, extrae un piojo de su axila, mátalo<br>¿Con qué valor hablar del psicoanálisis?<br><br>Otro ha entrado en mi pecho con un palo en la mano<br>¿Hablar luego de Sócrates al médico?<br><br>Un cojo pasa dando el brazo a un niño<br>¿Voy, después, a leer a André Bretón?<br><br>Otro tiembla de frío, tose, escupe sangre<br>¿Cabrá aludir jamás al Yo profundo?<br><br>Otro busca en el fango huesos, cáscaras<br>¿Cómo escribir, después del infinito?<br><br>Un albañil cae de un techo, muere y ya no almuerza<br>¿Innovar, luego, el tropo, la metáfora?<br><br>Un comerciante roba un gramo en el peso a un cliente<br>¿Hablar, después, de cuarta dimensión?<br><br>Un banquero falsea su balance<br>¿Con qué cara llorar en el teatro?<br><br>Un paria duerme con el pie a la espalda<br>¿Hablar, después, a nadie de Picasso?<br><br>Alguien va en un entierro sollozando<br>¿Cómo luego ingresar a la Academia?<br><br>Alguien limpia un fusil en su cocina<br>¿Con qué valor hablar del más allá?<br><br>Alguien pasa contando con sus dedos<br>¿Cómo hablar del no-yó sin dar un grito?</p>
  <p>(5 November, 1937)</p>
  <p><b>A man walks by with a loaf of bread on his shoulder</b></p>
  <p>&nbsp;&nbsp; A man walks by with a loaf of bread on his shoulder,<br>I’m going to write, after that, about my double?<br><br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Another sits, scratches, gets a louse out of his armpit,<br>cracks it. How dare one speak about psychoanalysis?<br><br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Another has entered my chest with a stick in his hand.<br>After that chat with the doctor about Socrates?<br><br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A cripple walks by arm in arm with a child.<br>After that I’m going to read Andre Breton?<br><br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Another shakes from cold, hacks, spits blood.<br>Is it possible to even mention the profound I?<br><br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Another searches in the mud for bones, rinds.<br>How write after that about the infinite?<br><br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A bricklayer falls from the roof, dies, and no longer eats lunch.<br>After that innovate the trope, the metaphor?<br><br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A merchant cheats a customer out of a gram.<br>After that talk about the fourth dimension?<br><br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A banker falsifies his balance.<br>With what face to cry in the theatre?<br><br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;An outcast sleeps with his foot behind his back.<br>After that, not talk about Picasso?<br><br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Someone goes to a burial sobbing.<br>How then enter the Academy?<br><br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Someone cleans a rifle in his kitchen.<br>How dare one speak about the beyond?<br><br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Someone walks by counting on his fingers.<br>How speak of the not-I without crying out?</p>
  <p>(translated from the Spanish by Clayton Eshleman)</p></blockquote>
