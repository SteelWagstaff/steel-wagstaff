> It’s a pleasure to spend the morning sitting in the shade, / where the stink of oil’s cut with the smell of green, / of tobacco, of wine, and where work comes to them, / right to the door.

— Cesare Pavese, &ldquo;<a href="http://www.poetryfoundation.org/poem/181986" target="_blank">Atlantic Oil</a>,&rdquo; trans. Geoffrey Brock.
