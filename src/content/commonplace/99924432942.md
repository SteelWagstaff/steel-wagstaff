It’s a pleasure to spend the morning sitting in the shade, / where the stink of oil’s cut with the smell of green, / of tobacco, of wine, and where work comes to them, / right to the door.                    — Cesare Pavese, “Atlantic Oil,” trans. Geoffrey Brock.
