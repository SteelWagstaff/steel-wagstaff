  <p style=\"\">Dear Students,</p>
  <p>I was a kid growing up in a troubled household. We didn’t have 
  books in the house but we did have the daily paper and I remember 
  picking out Family Circus before I could really read.</p>
  <p>
   There was something about the life on the other side of that circle 
  that looked pretty good. &nbsp;For kids like me there was a map and a compass
   hidden in Family Circus. The parents in that comic strip really loved 
  their children. Their home was stable. It put that image in my head and I
   kept it.<br><br> I’d always heard that great art will cause people to 
  burst into tears but the only time it ever happened to me was when I was
   introduced to Bil Keane’s son, Jeff. &nbsp;As soon as I shook his hand I 
  just started bawling my face off because I realized &nbsp;I had climbed 
  through the circle. </p>
  <p>And how I did it was by making pictures and writing stories. To me the Family Circus has always been my wished for family. My soul family in the image world. <br></p>
  <p>That’s why if you say a word against Family Circus to me if I’ve had a few drinks I will slug you so hard. </p>
  <p>You have heard me talk about the importance of comics for kids, and the kind of unexpected and sometimes lifesaving difference they can make— for me it was the comic “Family Circus”.&nbsp; <br></p>
  <p>I’ve seen that world through my side of the circle for over 50 years. This weekend at the 71st annual National Cartoonist’s Society Conference, I found out that Jeff Keane inked me into life INSIDE that circle.</p>
  <p style=\"\">SEE ABOVE!!!<br></p>
  <p>COMICS ARE MIRACULOUS!!! They are&nbsp; IMMUNE SYSTEMS! They are TRANSPORT SYSTEMS!!!&nbsp; They are TIME TRAVELING DEVICES!!</p>
  <p>Jeff asked a friend of mine why I was so in love with Family Circus.</p>
  <p>He said,“It’s not a ‘cool’ comic strip. <br></p>
  <p>Dearest Jeffy K,<br></p>
  <p style=\"\">Know this: Love is ALWAYS cool.</p>
  <p style=\"\">XOX</p>
  <p>Cousin Lynda B.<br></p>

  </blockquote>
