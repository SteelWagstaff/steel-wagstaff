And, though David thought the service itself somewhat irksome, he felt for that half hour, standing among his fellow Jews and praying with them, drawn to them all, every one, in a brotherhood against the unjust and cruel world. Leaving them, he found himself thinking of Genesis and putting into neat couplets, to be copied into his notebook when he had the chance: These are those near and dear yet – strangers except for blood – haters and traitors! Sit here, Simon and Levi, Reuben and the rest, sit down and feast. I am your brother Joseph.                    — Charles Reznikoff, The Lionhearted
