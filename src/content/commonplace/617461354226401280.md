> A Leaf A leaf, one of the last, parts from a maple branch:it is spinning in the transparent air of October, fallson a heap of others, stops, fades. No oneadmired its entrancing struggle with the wind,followed its flight, no one will distinguish it nowas it lies among other leaves, no one sawwhat I did. I am the only one.– Bronisław Maj, translated from the Polish by Czesław Miłosz & Robert Hass
