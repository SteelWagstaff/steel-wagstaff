> All things become islands before my senses,<br/> which accept them as a matter of course: a murmur of silence.<br/> All things in this darkness—I can know all of them,<br/> just as I know that blood flows in my veins.<br/> The plain is a great flowing of water through plants,<br/> a supper of all things. Each plant, and each stone,<br/> lives motionlessly. I hear my food feeding my veins<br/> with each living thing that this plain provides.

— Third stanza of Cesare Pavese, &ldquo;<a href="https:::::::://www.poetryfoundation.org/poem/180248" target="_blank">Passion for Solitude</a>&rdquo;, trans. Geoffrey Brock. Love this poem.
