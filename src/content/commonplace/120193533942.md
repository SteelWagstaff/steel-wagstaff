<p>Here is the pebble<br/>
It remained stubborn in itself<br/>
Neither on earth nor in heaven</p>

<p>It listens to itself<br/>
Among the worlds a world</p>                    — from Vasko Popa’s “Dream of the Pebble,” translated from the Serbian by Charles Simic.
