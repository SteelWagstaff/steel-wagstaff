> It is much pleasanter to give a rich man a dinner than a poor man. A poor man is grateful for ever morsel: he looks at you with a dog’s eyes: May I have this? even this? You say, of course. Please help yourself, but his gratitude is embarrassing. But a rich man eats slowly, savoring every bite and his praise is truly praise.                    
— Charles Reznikoff, handwritten note.
