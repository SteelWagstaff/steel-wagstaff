Aestheticism [was] the Genteel Tradition in American that William James … broke by insisting on ambulation, the finding of ideas in experience (not Ideas above it, as in saltation) and their employment in ongoing experience. … These terms from William James’ letters guide the study of two great writers who had learned that “knowing as it exists concretely” is processual, an open-ended activity, “ a continual event.”                    — Sherman Paul, Hewing to Experience
