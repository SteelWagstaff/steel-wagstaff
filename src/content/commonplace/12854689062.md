> Aestheticism [was] the Genteel Tradition in American that William James &hellip; broke by insisting on ambulation, the finding of ideas in experience (not Ideas above it, as in saltation) and their employment in ongoing experience. &hellip; These terms from William James&rsquo; letters guide the study of two great writers who had learned that &ldquo;knowing as it exists concretely&rdquo; is processual, an open-ended activity, &ldquo; a continual event.&rdquo;

— Sherman Paul, _Hewing to Experience_
