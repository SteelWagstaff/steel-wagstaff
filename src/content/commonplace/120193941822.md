That’s so much better  
We’ve saved ourselves from the flesh

Now we’ll do what we’ll do  
Say something

Would you like to be  
The backbone of lightning

Say a bit more

What can I say  
Hipbone of a storm

Say something else

That’s all I know  
Ribs of heaven

We’re nobody’s bones  
Say something else
