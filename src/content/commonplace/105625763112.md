> A newspaper was advertising the life of Huey Long, the dead spellbinder.<br/> The poster should have been taken down, for the story had been finished for months.<br/> Two thumb tacks kept the cardboard with his picture from blowing away;<br/> one was driven through his mouth,<br/> and its flat head looked like a seal.

— Charles Reznikoff
