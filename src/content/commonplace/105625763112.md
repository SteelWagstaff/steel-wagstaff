A newspaper was advertising the life of Huey Long, the dead spellbinder.
The poster should have been taken down, for the story had been finished for months.
Two thumb tacks kept the cardboard with his picture from blowing away;
one was driven through his mouth,
and its flat head looked like a seal.                    — Charles Reznikoff
