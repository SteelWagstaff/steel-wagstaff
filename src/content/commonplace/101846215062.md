> The world is always like a round, rolling eye, <br/> round and rolling since it existed: a cure for pain<br/> and then again a pain that supplants the cure.

— Abu'abdulla Ja'far bin Mahmud Rudaki, translated by Basil Bunting. In <em><a href="http://www.floodeditions.com/bunting-persia" target="_blank">Bunting&rsquo;s Persia</a></em>.<em><br/></em>
