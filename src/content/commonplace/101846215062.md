> The world is always like a round, rolling eye, <br/> round and rolling since it existed: a cure for pain<br/> and then again a pain that supplants the cure.

— Abu'abdulla Ja'far bin Mahmud Rudaki, translated by Basil Bunting. In _<a href="https://www.floodeditions.com/bunting-persia" target="_blank">Bunting&rsquo;s Persia</a>_._<br/>_
