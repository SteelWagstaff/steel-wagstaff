1.  
      The beginning of art—  
a rice-planting song  
      in the backcountry.

– Basho  

2.  
       In the thicket’s shade  
a woman by herself   
       singing the rice-planting song.

– Issa

3.  
“A Literary Note”

Once upon a time  
in a country without a written language  
a woman sang a song to her child.  
In its grandeur  
and melancholy  
and simplicity  
it surpassed all poems ever in books,  
even in the nine lost books of Sappho.  

Though this cannot be proven,  
I swear it happened.

– Naomi Replansky
