> “The Problem of Describing Trees”The aspen glitters in the windAnd that delights us.The leaf flutters, turning,Because that motion in the heat of AugustProtects its cells from drying out. Likewise the leafOf the cottonwood.The gene pool threw up a wobbly stemAnd the tree danced. No.The tree capitalized.No. There are limits to saying,In language, what the tree did.It is good sometimes for poetry to disenchant us.Dance with me, dancer. Oh, I will.Mountains, sky,The aspen doing something in the wind.– Robert Hass, from Time and Materials
