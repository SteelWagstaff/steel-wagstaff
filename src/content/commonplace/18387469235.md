To me the use of language means communication. If you write, you write to be understood, and if you’re not understood, you’ve failed. I’m a great believer in clarity and I try to practice it; that’s why I’m interested in precision in the use of words, which is a part of clarity. I think the only importance in writing is to convey meaning or emotion.                    — Charles Reznikoff
