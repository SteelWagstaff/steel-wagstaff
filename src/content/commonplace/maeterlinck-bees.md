Must we not admire the manner in which the thought or the god that the bees obey is at once revealed by their edifices, wrought with such striking conviction, by their customs and laws, their political and economical organization, their virtues, and even their cruelties? ... **The god of the bees is the future.** ... Have we often encountered an ideal more comformable to the desires of the universe, more widely manifest, more disinterested or sublime: have we often discovered an abnegation more complete and heroic?

-- Maurice Maeterlinck, <a href="https://www.ibiblio.org/eldritch/mm/b2.html">_The Life of the Bee_</a> (1901), translated from the French by Alfred Sutro
