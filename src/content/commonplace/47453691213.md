> Human existence is difficult to obtain. &hellip; Encountering good people is difficult to obtain. True spiritual friends are difficult to obtain, and attraction to the instruction regarding true conduct is difficult to obtain. A right livelihood in the world of men is difficult to obtain.

— Santideva, quoting from the <em>Gandavyuha</em> in his<em> Siksasamuccaya</em>
