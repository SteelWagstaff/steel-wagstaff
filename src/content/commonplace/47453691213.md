Human existence is difficult to obtain. … Encountering good people is difficult to obtain. True spiritual friends are difficult to obtain, and attraction to the instruction regarding true conduct is difficult to obtain. A right livelihood in the world of men is difficult to obtain.                    — Santideva, quoting from the Gandavyuha in his Siksasamuccaya
