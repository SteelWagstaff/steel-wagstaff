> How to Touch a Naked Man I’m at dissertation boot camp, and just got up from a long stint of uninterrupted writing to get some trail mix and a drink of water, to stretch my legs and converse with a friend. Walking back to the room I’m writing in, I passed a card stuck to the wall with an advertisement for Cosmo magazine with the words “How to Touch a Naked Man” written in all caps and bold black font prominently displayed and I imagined, or tried to imagine, the author of such a piece of writing. I ask this question in all earnestness: How is a person supposed to feel about themselves, knowing that they have written, for money, an article entitled “How to Touch an Naked Man,” for a publication like Cosmopolitan? What kind of feelings about the self are possible in such a scenario?
