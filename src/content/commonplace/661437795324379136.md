A trumpet like a sharp plow  
tills the night: how long  
till sunlight breaks the ground?

how long till those who listen respectfully  
turn around and see us?  
how long till we  
through effort and exertion   
turn to glory?

till the grain goes into the granary  
this thought belongs to no one  
a drop in the water-level between  
this moment and the next life:  
huge waves beat against the shore  
next door to youth  
we hear the wild palpitations

in a space even vaster  
sleep stuffed with rice straw

*translated by Eliot Weinberger and Iona Man-Cheong*
