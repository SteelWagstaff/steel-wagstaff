> Ultimately it&rsquo;ll come down to silence or sound, words or no words. And where are you going to get them? Where does language come from? Are you just going to make it out of a mouthful of air? Sometimes, but most of the time you don&rsquo;t; there&rsquo;s a world already there; it might be a poetic form that is still useful.

— Louis Zukofsky in <a href="http://www.jstor.org/stable/1207761">an interview with L.S. Dembo</a> published in _Contemporary Literature_, 1968
