> In a society that has an investment in seeing women as imperfect men, the danger points will be those at which women reveal that they have an independent essence, an existence that is not, in fact, under male control, a power and authority that either challenges male authority, or, more dangerously, that is not simply a version of parody of maleness, but is specifically female.

— Stephen Orgel, _Impersonations: The Performance of Gender in Shakespeare&rsquo;s England_
