Scott and I just returned from the Supreme Court. There were thousands of people there - many there to support equality but many there to oppose it. Scott and I stood front and center in front of the pro-equality crowd as the mass of anti-equality folks stood just a few feet in front of us - staring us down as security guards paced between the two groups. I can’t help but take it personal - all of those people, banded together to belittle our love. It hurts. Whenever I meet people who think so little of who I am and who I love, it hurts. Scott reminded me a few times to not get arrested (only half kidding) and though my internal temper and emotions occassionally flared, I kept my cool - because Scott was there, holding my hand defiantly as people stood just feet in front of us with their ugly, hate-filled signs. I’m confident that this is a fight that we will win. I’m not sure when we will win it, but we will win it. Until then, I will try to keep my cool, not get arrested and hold Scott’s hand. Love is patient, love is kind.                    — Recent post by a Facebook friend and former housemate (raised Mormon, now living in a loving same-sex relationship).
