> Sabine Heinlein's Among Murderers Last night I started reading Sabine Heinlein’s just published book Among Murderers (UC Press, 2013), about the struggle faced by three men incarcerated for murder for much of their adult life in New York State as they sought to re-enter society following their release. It was a fantastic, compelling, engrossing, thoughtful, complex, nuanced book, very lucid, very readable, very perceptive. I read through most of the night and just finished it this morning. Can’t forget Adam, Bruce, and Angel, nor can I recommend the book strongly enough, particularly for anyone who works, teaches, visits, or thinks about prisons and incarceration in the United States. If you can’t afford to purchase the book, but have an account with a library who subscribes to EBL (the Electronic Book Library), you can check out an electronic copy of the book for a week. Read this book.
