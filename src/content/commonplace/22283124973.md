So, we’re at Alcatraz and I see these two wild birds that look like they’re making out–they squeak and raise their beaks to the sky and nuzzle each other–and then repeat it over and over. I get up close and start recording, and then almost immediately they do this. Probably my favorite animal performance I’ve ever seen. Amazing.
