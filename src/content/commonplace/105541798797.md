> Martincito also keeps coming by. Great tales. Wants to take us to the Bull fights, the cock fights, and the whore houses. I kept sort of dodging. Finally told him that my idea of a pleasant evening was to find some place where there are no desparate women, no men in immediate danger of being dis-emboweled, and no chickens fighting!<br/> &ndash; I said it was perhaps a difference in age, being kindly.

— George Oppen, in a 1960 letter to his daughter, Linda.
