> If I stand very attentive even on a smaller cliff, then how much more so on an enduring chasm of a thousand leagues. <br/> It is inappropriate for me to be at ease, thinking, &ldquo;Just today death will not arrive.&quot;The time when I will not exist is inevitable.<br/> Who can give me fearlessness? How shall I escape? I shall certainly not exist. Why is my mind at ease?

— Santideva, <em>A Guide to the Bodhisattva Way of Life</em>.
