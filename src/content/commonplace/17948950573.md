Everything here is the path of a responding that examines as it listens. Any path always risks going astray, leading astray. To follow such paths takes practice in going. Practice needs craft. Stay on the path, in genuine need, and learn the craft of thinking, unswerving, yet erring.                    — Martin Heidegger, from A Letter to a Young Student (1950), the epilogue to the essay “The Thing” published in Poetry, Language, Thought.
