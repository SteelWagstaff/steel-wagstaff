Sure, I’d love to do a ‘write-up’ about you–but I’m not right for that sort of job. You’re a giant in poetry–and I don’t know the first god-damned thing about poetry. “Yankee-Doodle” is the furthest I ever got in poetry. We’re real friends, but all I can say about your creative life is to say you hung around the Fifth Ave. library learning 'words’; you appreciated feminine pulchritude; you were bowled over by the first god-damned drink you took and couldn’t stop raving about it.                    — Psychotherapist Harmon S. “Sol” Ephron to Carl Rakosi, 5.15.77
