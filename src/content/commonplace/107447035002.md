> Sure, I&rsquo;d love to do a &lsquo;write-up&rsquo; about you&ndash;but I&rsquo;m not right for that sort of job. You&rsquo;re a giant in poetry&ndash;and I don&rsquo;t know the first god-damned thing about poetry. &ldquo;Yankee-Doodle&rdquo; is the furthest I ever got in poetry. We&rsquo;re real friends, but all I can say about your creative life is to say you hung around the Fifth Ave. library learning 'words&rsquo;; you appreciated feminine pulchritude; you were bowled over by the first god-damned drink you took and couldn&rsquo;t stop raving about it.

— Psychotherapist Harmon S. &ldquo;Sol&rdquo; Ephron to Carl Rakosi, 5.15.77
