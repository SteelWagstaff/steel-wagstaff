the alliance–  
ships and plants  

The take-for-granted bloom  
of our roadsides  
          Queen Anne’s Lace  
          Black Eyed Susans  
                       rode the sea  

“Specimens graciously passed  
between warring fleets”  

As when an old boat rots ashore  
itself once living plant  
                         it sprouts.
