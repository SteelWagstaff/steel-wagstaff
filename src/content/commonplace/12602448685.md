Is the world more? Is it / five or six deer together, standing in dusk / abstract, momentary; then startled, dissolved in / newer and newer rhythms, mauve hoofs, red nostrils / eyes unwary as first stars?                    — Daniel Berrigan, from “Moment” in No One Walks Waters
