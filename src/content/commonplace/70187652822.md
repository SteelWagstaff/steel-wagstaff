> Joseph Smith III's diary of his mother Emma Smith Bidamon's death Tuesday, April 22: Found Alex here at Nauvoo and Julia in care of mother. Mrs. Abercrombie doing the work of the house. Wednesday, April 23: … Mother apparently failing. … Slight rain. … Cherries in blossom. The old places looks lovely but oh! how desolate. Thursday, April 24: … Slept splendidly so still, so pleasant. Clouds over the sky this am. … Mother continues to fail. Food fails to stimulate her. Friday, April 25: … Mother fails more rapidly. Has taken no nourishment for some hours, her pulse grows feeble constantly. … her breath labored. Saturday, April 26: … Mother quite bad. Sunday, April 27: … Was up with mother till 4:30. She was very bad. Did not thing she would live till morning. Emma would die around 4:20 am on April 30, 1879. Journal source: Qtd. in Linda King Newell and Valeen Tippetts Avery's Mormon Enigma: Emma Hale Smith
