> How dependent I am on analogy for understanding! Like the scientist. <br/> * * *<br/> It I look at anything long and hard, I wind up seeing only its inaccessibility, the mystery of its nature. <br/> But is the mystery really out there or in my own nature?<br/> * * *<br/> I am not altogether honest when I write. I find myself giving up my intentions for beauty of phrase. <br/> * * *<br/> I would like to write a play but I have no wish to move people around. My heart goes out to them the way they are. I want only to experience and cohabit them. <br/> And I have no interest in entertaining anybody. <br/> * * *<br/> How intractable the subject! But that only gives it depth and mystery, which are all I need to feel lyrical. <br/> * * * <br/> I’ve never had a really good idea that didn’t start with feeling. And a really good image has never come to me except by accident. My destiny lies in the hands of these two.<br/> * * * <br/> Only honesty and compassion, o imagination, can make you habitable.

— fragments from the Daybook section of Carl Rakosi's <em>Ex Cranium, Night.</em>
