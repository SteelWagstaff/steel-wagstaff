How dependent I am on analogy for understanding! Like the scientist. 
* * *
It I look at anything long and hard, I wind up seeing only its inaccessibility, the mystery of its nature. 
But is the mystery really out there or in my own nature?
* * *
I am not altogether honest when I write. I find myself giving up my intentions for beauty of phrase. 
* * *
I would like to write a play but I have no wish to move people around. My heart goes out to them the way they are. I want only to experience and cohabit them.  
And I have no interest in entertaining anybody. 
* * *
How intractable the subject! But that only gives it depth and mystery, which are all I need to feel lyrical. 
* * * 
I’ve never had a really good idea that didn’t start with feeling. And a really good image has never come to me except by accident. My destiny lies in the hands of these two.
* * * 
Only honesty and compassion, o imagination, can make you habitable.                    — fragments from the Daybook section of Carl Rakosi's <em>Ex Cranium, Night.</em>
