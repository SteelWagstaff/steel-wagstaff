> from Michel Pastoureau’s The Devil’s Cloth: A History of Stripes and Striped Fabric [translated by Jody Gladding] In fact, striped fabric is very much subject to the constraints of weaving methods, and its popularity, in this or that period, in whatever area, using whatever medium, must also be seen in relation to the history of techniques. There is no doubt that the increasing mechanization of thread and cloth production beginning from the 1770s … helped to expand the use of striped cloth in clothes, furniture, and decoration. Always and everywhere, the technical and the symbolic go hand in hand.
