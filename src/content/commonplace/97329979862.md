> But oh alas, so long, so far,<br/> Our bodies why do we forbear? &hellip;<br/> We owe them thanks, because they thus<br/> Did us, to us, at first convey,<br/> Yielded their senses&rsquo; force to us,<br/> Nor are dross to us, but allay. &hellip;<br/> To'our bodies turn we then, that so<br/> Weak men on love reveal&rsquo;d may look;<br/> Love&rsquo;s mysteries in souls do grow,<br/> But yet the body is his book.

— John Donne, &ldquo;<a href="http://www.poetryfoundation.org/poem/173355" target="_blank">The Ecstasy</a>&rdquo;
