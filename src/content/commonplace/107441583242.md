<p>Could a caring society ever be? </p>

<p>Recently a Nova production stated that wolves over a millenium or more years ago evolved into dogs, household members seeking love.</p>

<p>In time, in time could the human species similarly evolve? No more killings?</p>

<p>Growing old, I am growing old.</p>                    — Maurice Bernstein, in a letter to Carl Rakosi.
