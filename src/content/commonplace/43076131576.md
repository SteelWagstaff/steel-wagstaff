**Patient:**
But how did I get this [conjunctivitis]? Nobody farted in my eye.

**Heather [friend who works as a Physician's Assistant]:**
Excuse me?

**Patient:**
You know like they say in the movies/shows ... well, nobody farted in my eye

**Heather:**
what movie is that from?

**Patient:**
Well, do you have kids? 

**Heater:**
Yes

**Patient:**
Go home and ask your kids where they think pink eye comes from...they'll tell you it means somebody farted in their face.

**Heather:**
(Puzzled and not really feeling the need to explain that Charlotte can't even talk to explain this sort of knowledge): hmm...I have never heard that before

**Patient:**
Yeah, just ask anyone under the age of 40 and they will tell you that pink eye comes from fecal matter in the eye.
