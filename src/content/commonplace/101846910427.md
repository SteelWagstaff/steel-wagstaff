Dont hang an arse,
dont let your own dust overtake you,
nor the wind either,
get to Faridun before the wind. Put
everything else but this journey out of your head.
Dont get down till you come to the castle,                    — Salm giving directions to a messenger sent to Faridun, his father. Ferdowsi, translated by Basil Bunting, in Bunting’s Persia. And I thought ’driving like Jehu’ was moving furiously. I only wished the priest had been named!
