> I met a doctor, no longer young, who spoke of the desire to live among the old&ndash;even the incurably sick. When he was young, he told me, he was an interne in a hospital for the incurable. &ldquo;Why do you want to live?&rdquo; he asked them once. (I suppose only a very young man would have been inconsiderate enough to ask the question.) One of them answered, &ldquo;To see the daylight.&rdquo;

— Charles Reznikoff, &ldquo;Character Sketches in Prose&rdquo;
