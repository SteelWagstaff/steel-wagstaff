I met a doctor, no longer young, who spoke of the desire to live among the old–even the incurably sick. When he was young, he told me, he was an interne in a hospital for the incurable. “Why do you want to live?” he asked them once. (I suppose only a very young man would have been inconsiderate enough to ask the question.) One of them answered, “To see the daylight.”                    — Charles Reznikoff, “Character Sketches in Prose”
