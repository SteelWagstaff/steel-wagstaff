> from John Martone’s So Long Life a cloud of suds from lake shore – solider than soap bubbles – size & shape of some internal organ (why it drew you) & carry home, & place a speck of that cloud under your microscope.It’s a broken jewel world of algal parts – cell walls, shell slivers, rods, who knows, nothing you can name for sure – a chaos cloud from the larger one, & you look, not looking to know a thing, some latin binomial, just look. There’s all, an infinitesmal beauty out of this world, & you haven’t a clue. That’s fine, this end. It’s the lake where you almost drowned.
