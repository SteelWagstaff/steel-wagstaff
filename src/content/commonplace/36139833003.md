> Interview with George Oppen, conducted by Charles Amirkhanian and David Gitin, March 30, 1970 GO: We saw it [Paris in the early 30s] as already stale, already defeatist. Therefore we liked Brancusi who talked about hewing wood and-- And we are vaguely anti-art, are quite strongly anti-art. CA: But your wife's also an artist. GO: Yes, and has always written poetry too, from when we first met. DG: When you say anti-art, you mean against the cultism, the social field of art? GO: Yes, I suppose so. Maybe I'm only angry that not everything is art. CA: George, is there anything else that you'd like to say? GO: No. There wasn't anything I wanted to say to begin with. [Laughter from others--end of interview]
