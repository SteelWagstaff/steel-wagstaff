Let men in their madness blast every city on earth into black rubble and envelop the entire planet in a cloud of lethal gas–the canyons and hills, the springs and rocks will still be here, the sunlight will filter through, water will form and warmth shall be upon the land and after sufficient time, … living things will emerge. On this bedrock of animal faith I take my stand.                    — Edward Abbey, Desert Solitaire
