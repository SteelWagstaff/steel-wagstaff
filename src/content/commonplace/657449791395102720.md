To understand things, you have to understand the relationship between things … Once, the highest image of interrelationship in society was indicated by the word “God” and man was allowed to use earth and universal space under the condition that man should care for what he used. But we have to actualise this kind of care and respect since man came by his awareness nearer the phenomenon of interrelationship called the relation of atoms. Man became aware of his being part of a total energy system                    — Dutch architect J.B. Bakema in a 1974 lecture. Quoted in <i>Thoughts About Architecture</i>
