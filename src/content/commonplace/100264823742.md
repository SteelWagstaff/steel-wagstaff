> O luce, / chiarezza lontana, respiro / affannoso, rivolgi gli occhi / immobili e chairi su noi.

— <p>_O light, / distant clarity, difficult / breath, turn again your eyes / motionless and clear, to us._</p>
<p>Cesare Pavese</p>
