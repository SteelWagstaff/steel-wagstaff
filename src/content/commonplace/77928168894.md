I’m so impressed with this enhanced field research project currently being piloted at UW-Madison. David Gagnon and his team have built a ‘field guide’ app to enhance or replace the use of print-exclusive field guides  for plant identification activities in Seth McGee’s biocore classes. Students using the app are more accurate and more efficient than students merely using the field guide.
