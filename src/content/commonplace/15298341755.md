> <p>You need not see what someone is doing<br/> to know if it is his vocation,</p> <p><br/> you have only to watch his eyes:<br/> a cook mixing a sauce, a surgeon</p> <p><br/> making a primary incision,<br/> a clerk completing a bill of lading,</p> <p><br/> wear the same rapt expression,<br/> forgetting themselves in a function.</p> <p><br/> How beautiful it is,<br/> that eye-on-the-object look.</p>

— W.H. Auden, beginning of section I of &ldquo;Sext&rdquo; from &ldquo;<a href="http://www.vladivostok.com/speaking_in_tongues/auden9eng.htm">Horae Canonicae</a>&quot;
