> I have abandoned my intention of getting the B.Sc. Econ: I am now attempting to earn my living in Paris. If any part of my fees is returnable, please send it to me here.<br/> My reasons for abandoning the course are:<br/> (I) It interfered with my pursuit of literature.<br/> (II) It did not seem to lead to any secure employment.<br/> (III) It ceased to interest me for its own sake.<br/> (IV) It appeared (to me) to have turned several intelligent me into dullards, &amp; I did not desire a similar fate.<br/> I am now seeking employment as a bargee.

— Basil Bunting, letter to the secretary at the London School of Economics, 23 April 1923, quoted in <em>A Strong Song Tows Us</em><em>.</em>
