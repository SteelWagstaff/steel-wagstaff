Two scraps of paper with notes on floating around on my desk in the breeze from the window (hot here). One says: The best of the old lit. is as modern as the best of the modern. Other one says: Birds have lice almost all the time. So there you have me up to date.                    — Letter from Lorine Niedecker to Louis Zukofsky, July 7, 1946
