  <p><b>This Year in War 2015</b><br></p>
  <p>In the spirit of the season –– and by that I mean reminiscing about what a garbage fire this year has been and hoping against reason that next year will be different –– here is all* of the war that happened in 2015. If anything, I hope this offers a sense of what is currently in motion, where the great risks lie and which dilemmas are shaping strategic responses as we start a new year. I also hope this <a href=\"http://www.conflictmap.org/\">wide-angle view</a> can show some of the extent of the interplay among conflicts and the depth of their consequences, particularly for civilians.</p>
  <p>A note on format: because of the size and density, the piece is broken up into sections for easier or more selective reading. The categorization is imperfect. Regardless of which parts you do read, be sure to still scroll all the way down to find some of the best longform pieces on war-related subjects.</p>
  <p>*most/a lot of it.<br><br><a href=\"http://www.reuters.com/news/picture/downtime-on-the-frontline?articleId=USRTR4RWI5\">Photo</a>: North of Aleppo, Syria. February. A mud-stained fighter with Jabhat al-Shamiya rests with his weapon. Reuters.&nbsp;<br><br><b>NORTH AFRICA</b></p>
  <ul><li>The Malian government signed a <a href=\"http://www.aljazeera.com/news/2015/06/malian-rivals-sign-peace-deal-150620173301883.html\">peace deal</a> with Tuareg-led rebels in a deal brokered by Algeria. Despite this deal, Mali still faces a northern expanse rife with extremism as Islamist militants <a href=\"http://newirin.irinnews.org/dataviz/2015/11/20/map-of-conflict-in-mali-2015\">ramp up attacks</a>. (This article <a href=\"https://theconversation.com/limpasse-du-contre-terrorisme-au-sahel-52171\">breaks down the geography</a> of different militant groups operating in Mali.) The negotiations appear to have failed to stabilize the situation and experts <a href=\"http://www.liberation.fr/planete/2015/06/03/la-situation-au-mali-a-empire_1321971\">warn</a> that militant groups are reorganizing.<br></li>
  <li>Mali’s north has <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/world/2015/nov/25/the-struggle-for-mali\">become</a> a “vast, lawless expanse at the centre of west Africa; a crossroads for drugs, people and arms that links territories held by Boko Haram in Niger and Nigeria to the bases of veteran jihadists in Libya and Algeria.”<br></li>
  <li>Last month, the Radisson Blu Hotel in Mali’s capital, Bamako, was <a href=\"http://www.bbc.com/news/world-africa-34879955\">taken over</a> by gunmen. 170 people were taken hostage, and nineteen people were killed. Many questions remain unanswered about the source of the attack. It has been claimed by Al Qaeda in the Maghreb (AQIM) and by the extremist group <a href=\"http://www.longwarjournal.org/archives/2015/07/former-egyptian-special-forces-officer-leads-al-murabitoon.php\">Al Mourabitoun</a> (an Al Qaeda-linked group operating in North and West Africa, formed in 2013 in a merger of two other groups) Northern separatists groups in Mali say that the real target of the Radisson attack was the <a href=\"http://www.bigstory.ap.org/article/29ad97685baf4fcf869979c631c00746/mali-hotel-attack-aimed-peace-talks-say-separatists\">peace process</a> between the government and the north.<br></li>
  <li>The leader of Al Mourabitoun, Algerian militant Mokhtar Belmokhtar, was claimed killed by a US airstrike in eastern Libya, but AQIM have <a href=\"https://news.yahoo.com/militant-group-denies-u-air-strike-killed-algerian-203400683.html\">said</a> he is alive and well.<br></li>
  <li>Algeria’s long-serving intelligence chief, General Mohammed Mediene, was <a href=\"http://bigstory.ap.org/article/60cb5d8144bc4035b1079be4fd2fcece/algeria-removes-top-intel-chief-after-years-powerful-post\">pushed into retirement</a> after 25 years in office. Algeria’s former counter-espionage chief, Abdelkader Ait Ouarabi, is <a href=\"http://www.voanews.com/content/ap-algerias-ex-counterespionage-chief-to-stand-trial/3074629.html\">facing a military tribunal</a> on charges of destroying documents and breaching orders.<br></li>
  <li>The Islamic State has gained significant ground in Libya, a <a href=\"http://www.spiegel.de/international/world/islamic-state-uses-libya-to-gain-african-foothold-a-1066548.htmlhttp://www.spiegel.de/international/world/islamic-state-uses-libya-to-gain-african-foothold-a-1066548.html\">foothold</a> for the group in North Africa. Here, a citizen <a href=\"http://www.spiegel.de/international/world/the-islamic-state-diary-a-chronical-of-life-in-libyan-purgatory-a-1051422.html#ref=rss\">chronicles</a> life caught between the Islamic State and Al Qaeda in the city of Derna. An airstrike in the city of Derna in November reportedly <a href=\"http://www.aljazeera.com/news/2015/12/top-isil-leader-libya-killed-air-strike-151207171334252.html\">killed</a> Abu Nabil, the Islamic State’s leader inside Libya.<br></li>
  <li>Libya <a href=\"http://warisboring.com/articles/gaddafis-legacy-haunts-libya-and-the-world/\">after the fall of Gaddhafi</a> is in “<a href=\"http://www.economist.com/news/briefing/21638123-four-year-descent-arab-spring-factional-chaos-it-should-come\">factional chaos</a>,” finding itself yet again in a civil war as <a href=\"https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/worldviews/wp/2014/08/27/here-are-the-key-players-fighting-the-war-for-libya-all-over-again/\">multiple groups and governments</a> vie for power and territorial control. There is currently the internationally-recognized, Libyan National Army-backed “Tobruk” government, a Muslim Brotherhood-led government in Tripoli and its Libya Dawn coalition of Islamists, the Islamic State, and Salafist militant organization Ansar al-Sharia. Not to mention a host of smaller players and the ever-present <a href=\"http://www.nytimes.com/2015/11/13/world/middleeast/leaked-emirati-emails-could-threaten-peace-talks-in-libya.html?_r=0\">shadow</a> of <a href=\"http://nationalinterest.org/feature/how-the-gulf-arab-rivalry-tore-libya-apart-14580?platform=hootsuite\">foreign interference</a>. It is not just a highly volatile situation, but a highly confusing one. Oddly enough, one of the most detailed and updated explanations out there for what’s currently going on exists at the Wikipedia page for “<a href=\"https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_Civil_War_(2014%E2%80%93present)\">Libyan Civil War (2014 - present)</a>.”<br></li>
  <li>This month, the two rival parliaments in Libya initially <a href=\"https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/middle_east/libyas-rival-governments-shun-un-sign-separate-peace-deal/2015/12/06/0243b870-9c45-11e5-9ad2-568d814bbf3b_story.html\">rejected</a> a UN-brokered deal as foreign intervention but have just signed a <a href=\"http://www.aljazeera.com/news/2015/12/libya-rival-parliaments-sign-backed-deal-151217131709056.html\">unity agreement</a> in Morocco..</li>
  <li>There were two major attacks on foreign tourist targets in Tunisia. In March, 23 people were <a href=\"http://www.bbc.com/news/world-africa-31960926\">killed</a> at the Bardo Museum in Tunis. 20 of the dead were foreign tourists. In June, 39 people were killed in an <a href=\"http://www.bbc.com/news/world-africa-33287978\">attack</a> on beachgoers in the Tunisian resort city of Sousse. Analyst Aaron Zelin writes that the Sousse attacks represent a <a href=\"http://icsr.info/2015/07/icsr-insight-tunisian-libyan-jihadi-connection/\">longstanding and strengthening relationship</a> between Tunisian and Libyan militants.<br></li>
  <li>The group Sinai Province, which swore <a href=\"http://www.bbc.com/news/world-middle-east-25882504\">allegiance</a> to the Islamic State at the end of last year, has been waging an(other) <a href=\"http://www.bbc.com/news/world-middle-east-33905477\">insurgency</a> in the Sinai since current president Abdel Fattah al-Sisi’s coup against his predecessor, Mohammed Morsi.<br></li>
  <li>In October, a Russian passenger jet carrying 224 people <a href=\"https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/russian-airliner-crashes-in-egypts-sinai-peninsula/2015/10/31/feff65e0-7fa9-11e5-beba-927fd8634498_story.html\">crashed</a> on the Sinai Peninsula, with all on board dying. Russia concluded it to have been the result of terrorism, though Egypt <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/world/2015/dec/14/no-evidence-of-terrorism-sinai-air-crash-egypt-says?CMP=twt_gu\">disagrees</a>.<br></li>
  <li>Egypt’s top prosecutor, Hisham Barakat, was <a href=\"http://www.nytimes.com/2015/06/30/world/middleeast/roadside-bomb-injures-egypts-top-prosecutor.html?_r=1\">assassinated</a> this summer at the outset of a <a href=\"http://time.com/3969596/egypt-isis-sinai/\">series of militant assaults</a>. He is the most senior official to be killed in the ongoing militant insurgency.<br></li>
  <li>Egypt has responded to the heightened threat level by widening government powers and enacting harsh new <a href=\"http://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2015/08/19/human-rights-group-slams-egypts-new-anti-terrorism-law\">anti-terror legislation</a>. Its <a href=\"https://www.hrw.org/news/2015/08/19/egypt-counterterrorism-law-erodes-basic-rights\">repressive</a> and <a href=\"http://www.newsweek.com/2015/08/21/egypt-muslim-brotherhood-fate-361663.html\">heavy-handed</a> <a href=\"http://www.reuters.com/article/us-egypt-lawyers-special-report-idUSKCN0SG19E20151126#UwOfPbti7jyBmdzg.97\">crackdown</a> on Muslim Brotherhood supporters is thought to be <a href=\"http://www.theatlantic.com/international/archive/2015/08/egypt-sisi-terrorism-muslim-brotherhood/401792/\">driving radicalization and terror</a>, rather than stemming it.<br></li>
  <li>The US <a href=\"http://www.reuters.com/article/us-usa-egypt-military-idUSKBN0MR2GR20150401#GqtkbmXsoHrRLerV.97\">unfroze</a> military aid to Egypt in the spring. Aid and arms transfers had been halted as a consequence of the 2013 coup.<br></li>
  <li>As of June, the UN was saying that as many as 200,000 civilians had been <a href=\"http://www.nytimes.com/2015/06/11/world/africa/sudan-attacks-uproot-thousands.html?partner=rss&amp;emc=rss\">forced</a> to flee violence in Darfur. Human Rights Watch has collected <a href=\"http://www.sudantribune.com/spip.php?article54642\">evidence</a> that the government of Sudan used cluster munitions in civilian areas of South Kordofan.<br></li>
  <li>This fall, Human Rights Watch <a href=\"https://www.hrw.org/node/280756/\">released a report</a> on the devastating consequences of two military operations led by Sudan’s Rapid Support Forces: Operation Decisive Summer (late February - early May 2014) and Operation Decisive Summer II (January - June 2015). RSF reportedly displaced entire communities, destroyed sources of water and food, murdered and tortured civilians and carried out mass rapes in areas of Darfur with the aid of proxy militias and the Sudanese Air Force.<br></li>
  <li>The latest round of peace talks for conflict-ridden Darfur, South Kordofan and Blue Nile held in Addis Ababa have failed – <a href=\"https://news.yahoo.com/sudan-rebels-army-brace-fighting-season-peace-talks-102234805.html\">now Sudan is bracing for renewed fighting</a> as the rainy season ends.</li>
  </ul><p><b>SUBSAHARAN AFRICA</b></p>
  <ul><li>The Ivory Coast’s former president, Laurent Gbagbo, and his deputy, Charles Ble Goude, <a href=\"https://news.yahoo.com/former-ivory-coast-leader-gbagbo-fit-trial-icc-163432122.html\">will stand trial</a> this coming year for crimes against humanity over their role in 2010’s electoral violence.<br></li>
  <li>Sectarian violence has punished the <a href=\"http://www.bbc.com/news/world-africa-13150044\">Central African Republic</a> since 2013, when President François Bozizé (himself a former rebel leader who took power in 2006) was ousted by (mainly Muslim) Seleka rebel coalition. About 6000 people have died in the resulting sectarian conflict.<br></li>
  <li>Though rival fighting groups <a href=\"http://www.france24.com/en/20150511-central-african-republic-militias-seleka-anti-balaka-disarm-deal\">signed</a> a peace agreement in May, violence continues. T<a href=\"http://www.bbc.com/news/world-africa-13150044\">he country is still unsafe and politically precarious</a>. The UNHCR <a href=\"http://www.unhcr.org/5669a3a66.html\">reports</a> “insecurity and human rights abuses remain rife” with high rates of sectarian violence, and criminal enterprise <a href=\"http://allafrica.com/download/resource/main/main/idatcs/00091554:6138ad932a4ad3677b360aa72dbf116d.pdf\">continues</a> to fuel armed groups. As the peace process progresses, refugees <a href=\"http://www.irinnews.org/report/101780/a-tough-homecoming-in-central-african-republic\">returning home</a> are struggling to reassert the normalcy of everyday life. Many, like the Muslim population of the PK5 district of Bangui, the country’s capital city, are in forced isolation in sectarian enclaves and unable to move safely and freely.<br></li>
  <li>New <a href=\"http://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2015-12-08/central-african-republic-armed-group-says-it-won-t-allow-vote\">agitation</a> and unrest is stirring in the lead-up to new CAR elections on December 27th. The Constitutional Court recently <a href=\"http://allafrica.com/stories/201512090333.html\">rejected</a> Bozize’s bid for presidential candidacy, causing protests and gunfire in Bangui.<br></li>
  <li>The UN’s handling of a <a href=\"https://news.yahoo.com/expect-more-sex-abuse-cases-central-african-republic-155826418.html\">string of child sex abuse allegations</a> against MINUSCA peacekeeping forces in the CAR has been called “<a href=\"https://news.vice.com/article/the-un-completely-failed-sexually-abused-boys-in-the-central-african-republic\">seriously flawed</a>” by an independent review panel.</li>
  <li>Newly-elected Nigerian President Buhari has made crushing Boko Haram’s insurgency his top <a href=\"http://www.france24.com/en/20150713-nigeria-buhari-president-sacks-fires-defence-boko-haram\">priority</a>. Though Boko Haram remains incredibly deadly and dangerous, especially to civilians, its hopes of creating a territorial caliphate like the Islamic State have been significantly <a href=\"http://foreignpolicy.com/2015/12/08/boko-haram-wounded-and-dangerous-terrorism-nigeria/?wp_login_redirect=0\">disrupted</a> by Buhari’s regional military coalition, which has successfully taken back much of the group’s territory. &nbsp;(Documentary: “<a href=\"https://news.vice.com/video/the-war-against-boko-haram-full-length\">The War Against Boko Haram</a>,” Kaj Larsen for VICE News.)<br></li>
  <li>The campaign against Boko Haram is not without its own major problems. One being <a href=\"http://www.jeuneafrique.com/279746/politique/nigeria-corruption-larmee-lutte-contre-boko-haram/\">corruption</a>, and the other human rights violations. Amnesty International reported that 8,000 prisoners have <a href=\"http://www.reuters.com/article/us-nigeria-military-amnesty-idUSKBN0OJ1YD20150603#vzUc1APIoP6PSxd4.97\">died</a> in detention during the Nigerian military’s campaign against Boko Haram.<br></li>
  <li>The <a href=\"http://ig.ft.com/sites/2015/boko-haram-timeline/\">five-year-old group</a>, which <a href=\"http://www.reuters.com/article/us-nigeria-violence-islamicstate-idUSKBN0M82M620150313#H8kWGdDkYQAqVErt.97\">pledged</a> its allegiance to the Islamic State, has not limited itself to attacking Nigeria – but has conducted deadly attacks in Niger, Cameroon and <a href=\"http://www.france24.com/en/20151110-chad-declares-state-emergency-region-hit-boko-haram\">Chad</a>.<br></li>
  <li>The conflict in South Sudan is now <a href=\"http://sudantribune.com/spip.php?article57260\">two years old</a> and has a death toll in the tens of thousands. Most of the dead are civilians. More than 2 million have fled their homes – more than 1.5 million left displaced inside the country while 650,000 have crossed into other countries as refugees. Human Rights Watch released a <a href=\"https://www.hrw.org/news/2015/12/14/south-sudan-terrifying-lives-child-soldiers\">report</a> this month detailing the lives of the thousands of child soldiers in South Sudan and naming some of the commanders who have used them.<br></li>
  <li>In February, a <a href=\"http://www.nytimes.com/2015/02/02/world/africa/rebels-agree-to-cease-fire-in-south-sudan.html\">ceasefire</a> was agreed to between rivals Riek Machar and Salva Kiir, but fighting has gone on. In May, the UN <a href=\"http://www.france24.com/en/20150512-south-sudan-child-rapes-kidnappings\">sounded the alarm</a> about reports from South Sudan’s Unity state of extreme violence: burning villages, killing civilians and abductions and rapes and abductions of children. More such <a href=\"http://www.news24.com/Africa/News/129-children-killed-in-South-Sudan-fighting-Unicef-20150619\">horrifying reports</a> <a href=\"http://www.reuters.com/article/us-southsudan-war-crime-idUSKCN0PW1TH20150722#zVSsJBJ4BYLSboTD.97\">continued</a> to come out over the summer. The UN says that the <a href=\"http://www.nytimes.com/2015/08/26/world/africa/un-report-details-flow-of-foreign-weapons-into-south-sudan.html?partner=rss&amp;emc=rss\">high volume flow of weapons</a> into South Sudan has been a critical factor in ongoing violence.<br></li>
  <li>In the Democratic Republic of Congo, <a href=\"https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/africa/un-report-points-to-pre-election-rights-violations-in-congo/2015/12/08/7a3f8116-9df7-11e5-9ad2-568d814bbf3b_story.html\">pre-election human rights violations</a> are on the rise as incumbent President Joseph Kabila, barred from seeking a third term by the 2006 constitution, <a href=\"https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/monkey-cage/wp/2015/12/03/will-congo-vote-in-2016/\">attempts to extend his stay in power</a>.<br></li>
  <li>The DRC launched an <a href=\"http://www.reuters.com/article/us-congodemocratic-un-idUSKBN0O021C20150515#qcjFi1aeiOuyDy48.97\">offensive</a> in the east in February against the Democratic Forces for the Liberation of Rwanda (FDLR) rebel group in February. This offensive recently <a href=\"https://news.yahoo.com/one-main-instigators-rwandas-genocide-finally-nabbed-195538434.html\">captured</a> a longtime fugitive hiding with the FDLR – Ladislas Ntaganzwa – on the run for the past 21 years for his role in the 1994 Rwandan genocide.<br></li>
  <li>The country has been persistently under the shadow of conflict for decades, and, as <a href=\"http://www.cfr.org/congo-democratic-republic-of/eastern-congo/p37236?cid=otr-marketing_use-Congo_InfoGuide#!/\">this Council on Foreign Relations analysis</a> puts it, the east “continues to defy efforts at pacification” as <a href=\"http://www.reuters.com/article/us-congodemocratic-security-idUSKBN0TN21S20151205#W7cfLxgsd2y8Qszk.97\">local insurgent groups dig in</a>.<br></li>
  <li>At the beginning of April, the extremist group Al-Shabab attacked Garissa University in northeastern Kenya. 147 people were <a href=\"http://www.bbc.com/news/world-africa-32169080\">killed</a>, mostly students, in the group’s deadliest attack so far. (“<a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/world/2015/apr/02/al-shabaab-from-al-qaida-rejects-to-a-fighting-force-of-thousands?CMP=twt_gu\">Al Shabaab: from al-Qaeda rejects to a fighting force of thousands</a>,” by David Smith in The <i>Guardian</i>)<br></li>
  <li>Al Shabaab (who has allegiance to Al Qaeda) is trying to <a href=\"http://www.longwarjournal.org/archives/2015/10/shabaab-leadership-fights-islamic-state-expansion.php\">push back</a> against Islamic State expansionism in east Africa and has been <a href=\"http://www.newsweek.com/isis-or-al-qaeda-somalias-al-shabab-divided-over-allegiance-397772\">swift and unmerciful</a> with any signs of <a href=\"http://www.reuters.com/article/us-mideast-crisis-somalia-idUSKCN0SH1BF20151023#Q3sH4UbUgRe29koZ.97\">caliphate sympathies</a> within the ranks.<br></li>
  <li>Chad’s former dictator and US ally Hissène Habré went on <a href=\"http://www.spiegel.de/international/world/chad-dictator-hissene-habre-continues-in-dakar-a-1055069.html\">trial</a> in Dakar and was forced to <a href=\"http://www.stripes.com/news/africa/victims-of-torture-in-chad-anticipate-justice-as-dictator-s-trial-proceeds-1.371743\">face some of his victims</a>.<br></li>
  <li>When Burundi’s president Pierre Nkurunziza began to look as if he was going to run for a third five-year term, tensions began to <a href=\"http://www.reuters.com/article/us-burundi-politics-analysis-idUSKBN0NE0W020150423#RGQOWfVbEjrT5m9s.97\">climb</a>. In May, General Godefroid Niyombare <a href=\"http://www.france24.com/en/20150514-live-fighting-rages-burundi-day-after-attempted-coup\">attempted</a> a coup against Nkurunziza, but failed.<br></li>
  <li>As protests turned violent ahead of elections in June, tens of thousands of Burundians <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/global-development/2015/apr/30/burundi-election-violence-thousands-flee-rwanda-president-nkurunziza\">fled</a> to Rwanda. Ongoing electoral unrest in Burundi caused a <a href=\"http://www.nytimes.com/2015/06/05/world/africa/burundi-postpones-elections-amid-unrest.html?ref=world\">delay</a> in the elections, but did not <a href=\"http://www.france24.com/en/20150609-burundi-president-third-term-bid-non-negotiable-nkurunziza\">sway</a> Nkurunziza from seeking, and ultimately winning, a third term in office. After some high profile <a href=\"http://www.telegraph.co.uk/news/worldnews/africaandindianocean/burundi/11805287/Burundis-former-army-chief-shot-dead-in-second-political-assassination-this-month.html\">assassinations</a> of Nkurunziza <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/world/2015/aug/02/burundis-de-facto-internal-security-chief-killed-in-rocket-attack-on-car\">allies</a> following his victory, regime paranoia and resulting political murders and repression are <a href=\"http://www.vox.com/2015/11/13/9728166/burundi-ciris\">causing fear</a> of <a href=\"http://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/africa/burundi-election-president-pierre-nkurunzizas-victory-has-reignited-fears-of-genocide-like-that-a6734026.html\">possible genocide</a> and return to <a href=\"http://www.bbc.com/news/world-africa-34987198\">ethnic violence</a>. (Burundi’s civil, which ended in 2005 and took 300,000 lives was fueled by the same interethnic violence between Hutus and Tutsis that gave rise to genocide in neighboring Rwanda.) Hundreds have died this year, and just this month 87 people died in some of the worst violence since the failed coup. Intervention in the conflict is <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/world/2015/aug/06/burundi-african-union-assassination\">complicated</a> because the African Union relies on Burundi’s presence in Somalia.<br></li>
  <li>Niyombare wasn’t the only African general whose big plans went awry. In Burkina Faso in <a href=\"http://www.bbc.com/news/world-africa-34418006\">September</a>, General Gilbert Dienderé – <a href=\"http://www.dw.com/en/burkina-fasos-defiant-presidential-guard/a-18750822\">commander of the presidential guard</a> – <a href=\"http://www.bbc.com/news/world-africa-34318822\">attempted</a> his own coup against interim President Michel Kafando. It failed miserably, resulting in the dissolution of the presidential guard and <a href=\"http://www.dw.com/en/burkina-faso-charges-coup-leader-with-crimes-against-humanity/a-18787968\">charges of crimes against humanity</a> for Dienderé. The guard was a feared entity, and Burkina Faso is now debating what to do with its <a href=\"http://bigstory.ap.org/article/ad55d7394024476193f5ad40abc89297/burkina-faso-victims-seek-answers-former-torture-site\">headquarters</a> – a notorious site of torture.</li>

Bergdahl was charged with <a href=\"https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/checkpoint/wp/2015/09/17/bowe-bergdahls-commanding-officer-i-was-in-absolute-disbelief-that-i-couldnt-find-one-of-my-men/\">desertion</a> this year.<br></li>
  <li>Cuba was <a href=\"http://www.politico.com/story/2015/04/cuba-off-terrorism-list-116964\">removed</a> from the US list of state sponsors of terrorism as relations were re-established and normalized.<br></li>
  <li>An international panel of human rights experts found the US and Mexico to be jointly <a href=\"http://www.huffingtonpost.com/azadeh-shahshahani/tribunal-finds-mexico-and_b_8824162.html\">responsible</a> for the ongoing drug war. According to data released by the Mexican government, the drug war, which has <a href=\"https://www.washingtonpost.com/investigations/censor-or-die-the-death-of-mexican-news-in-the-age-of-drug-cartels/2015/12/09/23acf3ae-8a26-11e5-9a07-453018f9a0ec_story.html?hpid=hp_hp-more-top-stories_mexicopress-230pm%3Ahomepage%2Fstory\">stifled journalistic freedoms</a> in Mexico, <a href=\"http://www.pbs.org/wgbh/frontline/article/the-staggering-death-toll-of-mexicos-drug-war/\">claimed</a> 164,000 lives between 2007 and 2014.<br></li>
  <li>Even a year on from the <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/world/2015/sep/20/mexico-43-killed-students-\">mass killing</a> of 43 students in Guerrero, their families have no justice and no answers.</li>
  </ul><p><b>CENTRAL AND SOUTH AMERICA</b></p>
  <ul><li>A Honduran journalist <a href=\"http://www.motherjones.com/media/2015/11/honduras-journalist-violence-fear-san-pedro-sula\">recounted</a> her experiences as an investigative reporter in a country where 51 journalists have been murdered since 2003.<br></li>
  <li>Colombia is in the <a href=\"https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/the_americas/it-may-not-look-like-it-but-a-colombia-peace-agreement-could-be-within-reach/2015/08/03/5b7471e4-353b-11e5-b835-61ddaa99c73e_story.html\">process</a> of bringing to an end half a century of conflict with the FARC rebel group. <a href=\"https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/the_americas/colombias-rebels-want-peace-but-fear-giving-up-their-guns/2015/11/14/151d5e7a-7bf1-11e5-bfb6-65300a5ff562_story.html\">Laying down their weapons</a> and joining civilian life is <a href=\"https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/the_americas/an-end-to-colombias-war-seems-close--except-in-rebel-territory/2015/10/15/fa58c684-65dd-11e5-bdb6-6861f4521205_story.html\">not an easy move</a> for rebels, especially in rural areas, but <a href=\"http://www.nbcnews.com/news/world/colombia-farc-rebel-group-announce-major-breakthrough-peace-talks-n432646\">major breakthroughs</a> have been made at the negotiating table this year. One in particular is an <a href=\"http://www.bbc.com/news/world-latin-america-35103709\">agreement</a> on justice and reparations for the war’s victims and their families.<br></li>
  <li>Argentine prosecutor Alberto Nisman <a href=\"http://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2015/07/20/death-of-a-prosecutor\">alleged</a> that top officials, including President Cristina Fernandéz de Kirchner, took part in a conspiracy to cover up Iran’s part in 1994 suicide bombing of a Jewish cultural center. Nisman was found <a href=\"http://www.nytimes.com/2015/01/20/world/americas/alberto-nisman-found-dead-argentina-amia.html?_r=0\">dead</a> <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/world/2015/jan/28/alberto-nisman-death-cristina-fernandez-de-kirchner-argentina-intelligence-agency\">on the eve of his testimony</a> before lawmakers. <a href=\"http://www.bbc.com/news/magazine-32887939\">Foul play</a> was <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/world/2015/mar/05/alberto-nisman-argentina-president-fernandez\">immediately</a> and widely <a href=\"http://www.wsj.com/articles/new-twist-in-death-of-argentine-official-1433298094\">suspected</a><br></li>
  <li>
  <a href=\"http://www.npr.org/sections/goatsandsoda/2015/10/05/445382231/how-el-salvador-fell-into-a-web-of-gang-violence\">Warring gangs</a> MS-13 and Barrio 18 have <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/world/2015/apr/06/el-salvador-violence-end-to-gang-truce-proves-deadly\">plunged</a> El Salvador into <a href=\"http://www.newyorker.com/news/news-desk/the-executioners-of-el-salvador?mbid=rss\">extreme levels of violence</a>. The country of 6.3 million averaged 30 murders a day in August and roughly <a href=\"http://www.aljazeera.com/indepth/inpictures/2015/09/gang-wars-el-salvador-bloodiest-year-150914095306221.html\">700 or more people are killed a month</a>.</li>
  </ul><p><b>TERROR AT HOME/THE GLOBAL WAR ON TERROR</b></p>
  <ul><li>The Global Terrorism Index 2015 reports that 2014 saw an <a href=\"http://www.smh.com.au/world/terrorism-deaths-in-2014-the-highest-on-record-global-terrorism-index-2015-finds-20151119-gl2puz.html\">80% increase</a> in terrorism deaths from 2013, with 32,600 deaths. 78% of those deaths occurred in Afghanistan, Pakistan, Nigeria, Iraq and Syria.<br></li>
  <li>US arms manufacturers are feeling the <a href=\"http://www.reuters.com/article/us-mideast-crisis-usa-arms-insight-idUSKBN0TN2DA20151204#4lrv6EO22oW1fZyC.97\">strain</a> of high demand for smart bombs.<br></li>
  <li>The threat of “<a href=\"http://america.aljazeera.com/articles/2015/1/13/paris-attacks-affiliation.html\">freelance terrorism</a>” was a major feature of 2015 the United States. Sometimes the result of domestically-sourced motivations, as with the racially-motivated murders of 9 people in a church in <a href=\"http://www.cnn.com/2015/07/16/us/gallery/chattanooga-tennessee-shooting/index.html\">Charleston</a>, and other times overlapping with international extremism as with the recent killing spree in <a href=\"http://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/Multiple-Victims-Reported-in-San-Bernardino-Shooting-360080371.html\">San Bernardino, CA</a>, where 14 people died. In any year, these attacks and those in Paris would spur <a href=\"http://thehill.com/policy/finance/262135-scrutiny-falls-on-marriage-visas-after-san-bernardino-attack\">serious</a> and <a href=\"http://blogs.cfr.org/cyber/2015/12/08/cyber-policy-after-the-paris-and-san-bernardino-terrorist-attacks/\">long-ranging questions</a> about domestic and international policy, but this is an <a href=\"https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/after-san-bernardino-how-the-foreign-policy-debate-may-be-shifting-for-gop-candidates/2015/12/07/64a96da2-9a9c-11e5-8917-653b65c809eb_story.html\">election season</a>. The resulting rise in xenophobia and xenophobic policy suggestions as a result of Paris and San Bernardino has given <a href=\"http://www.nytimes.com/2015/12/11/world/europe/voter-insecurities-feed-rise-of-right-leaning-populist-politicians.html\">strength</a> to far&nbsp;<a href=\"https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/europe/frances-far-right-reaps-political-gains-as-fears-of-terrorism-grow/2015/11/24/d01838e8-92bd-11e5-befa-99ceebcbb272_story.html\">right movements</a>.<br></li>
  <li>Boston bomber Dzhokhar Tsarnaev was found <a href=\"http://www.cnn.com/2015/04/08/us/boston-marathon-bombing-trial/index.html\">guilty</a> on 30 counts, from using a weapon of mass destruction to bombing a place of public use. He was later <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/us-news/live/2015/may/15/dzhokhar-tsarnaev-boston-marathon-bombing\">sentenced</a> to death by lethal injection.</li>
  </ul><p><b>TORTURE AND DETENTION POLITICS</b></p>
  <ul><li>Guantánamo Bay inmate Mohamedou Ould Slahi’s <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/world/ng-interactive/2015/jan/20/-sp-read-mohamedou-ould-slahis-original-handwritten-manuscript\">memoir</a>, <a href=\"http://www.amazon.com/Guant%C3%A1namo-Diary-Mohamedou-Ould-Slahi/dp/0316328685\">Guantánamo Diary</a>, was published in January on the heels of the late-2014 release of the Senate Select Intelligence Committee’s report on the US use of torture.&nbsp;<br></li>
  <li>After 14 years in Guantánamo, British citizen Shaker Aamer, never charged with any crime, has been <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/world/2015/oct/30/shaker-aamer-released-from-guantanamo-bay\">released</a>. He gave an <a href=\"http://www.dailymail.co.uk/news/article-3357700/Guantanamo-Briton-Shaker-Aamer-relives-14-year-nightmare-savage-abuse-including-agony-hog-tied-45-minutes-time-joy-finally-seeing-family-again.html\">interview</a> to the <i>Daily Mail </i>chronicling his abuse and his joy at his freedom.<br></li>
  <li>A still-classified internal CIA review, known as the Panetta Report, <a href=\"http://www.nytimes.com/2015/01/21/world/cia-report-found-value-of-brutal-interrogation-was-inflated.html?ref=world\">found</a> that the agency exaggerated the effectiveness of torture techniques and overstated the worth of intelligence obtained from them.<br></li>
  <li>The American Psychological Association <a href=\"http://www.nytimes.com/2015/05/01/us/report-says-american-psychological-association-collaborated-on-torture-justification.html?partner=rss&amp;emc=rss\">collaborated</a> with the Bush administration to justify post-9/11 torture practices. Psychologist Jean Marie Arrigo, who warned of the APA’s role in the program and was the subject of collective disbelief and a smear campaign, has been <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/law/2015/jul/13/psychologist-torture-doctors-collusion-jean-maria-arrigo\">vindicated</a>.<br></li>
  <li>The Bureau of Investigative Journalism has painstakingly <a href=\"https://www.thebureauinvestigates.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/complete-v12.pdf\">profiled</a> the 119 prisoners who went through the CIA’s detention and interrogation program.<br></li>
  <li>A <i>Guardian</i> <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2015/feb/24/chicago-police-detain-americans-black-site\">investigation</a> into <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2015/mar/03/chicago-police-violence-homan-square\">Chicago police abuse</a> uncovered a domestic CIA-esque “black site” run by the Chicago PD at Homan Square, an off-the-books interrogation center where people were subjected to beatings, prolonged shackling, and denial of counsel. Those who were brought there were not booked, or officially logged in any way. Further investigation <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2015/oct/19/homan-square-chicago-police-disappeared-thousands\">revealed</a> that 7,000 people were disappeared inside the facility between 2004 and 2015 – about 6,000 of whom were black.<br></li>
  <li>The <i>Guardian</i> also reported, in a two-part story, that Chicago detective Richard Zuley, who went on to be involved in torture at Guantánamo, <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2015/feb/18/american-police-brutality-chicago-guantanamo\">began those brutal techniques on poor and minority Chicagoans</a> to <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2015/feb/19/chicago-police-richard-zuley-abuse-innocent-man\">extract confessions</a>.<br></li>
  <li>Omar Khadr, who was put in Guantánamo in 2002 at age 15 and transferred to his home country of Canada in 2012, <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/world/2015/may/07/canada-free-bail-omar-khadr-guantanamo-bay-youngest\">walked free </a>this year. The <i>Toronto Star </i><a href=\"http://projects.thestar.com/omar-khadr-in-his-own-words/index.html\">profiles</a> him.<br></li>
  <li>The US is now <a href=\"http://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2015/dec/01/mistaken-identity-guantanamo-bay-mustafa-al-aziz-al-shamiri\">admitting</a> that Mustafa al-Aziz al-Shamiri, who has been in Guantánamo for 13 years, was never a courier or trainer for al-Qaeda but a low-level Islamist foot soldier.<br></li>
  <li>Another of the facility’s “forever prisoners,” Zahir Hamdoun, held since 2002 – is seeking <a href=\"http://www.miamiherald.com/news/nation-world/world/americas/guantanamo/article48576320.html\">release</a> from the national security parole board.<br></li>
  <li>There have been a <a href=\"https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/national-security/moroccan-detainee-held-at-guantanamo-bay-for-more-13-years-is-repatriated/2015/09/17/c8dbe316-5d4a-11e5-b38e-06883aacba64_story.html\">number</a> of <a href=\"http://www.nytimes.com/2015/09/23/us/politics/us-repatriates-a-saudi-detainee-from-guantanamo.html?_r=0\">repatriations</a> this year as the Pentagon <a href=\"https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/national-security/facing-threat-in-congress-pentagon-races-to-resettle-guantanamo-inmates/2015/04/22/e001e328-e82a-11e4-9a6a-c1ab95a0600b_story.html\">moved quickly</a> against Congress’s plans to shut down transfers. In November, the Senate <a href=\"http://www.nytimes.com/2015/11/11/us/politics/senate-passes-military-bill-that-bars-transfers-of-guantanamo-detainees.html\">passed</a> a defense authorization bill that included provisions restricting transfers abroad and banning relocation of the prison’s detainees to prisons inside the US, for residency or for trial.<br></li>
  <li>Here, as of mid-September, is a <a href=\"http://www.miamiherald.com/news/nation-world/world/americas/guantanamo/article2203501.html\">list</a> of who is still held at the Guantánamo facility.<br></li>
  <li>Once again, the <i>Miami Herald</i>’s Carol Rosenberg has <a href=\"http://www.miamiherald.com/news/nation-world/world/americas/guantanamo/\">provided</a> us with the best, most comprehensive and solidly reported journalism on the goings on at Guantánamo, from transfers to trials.</li>
