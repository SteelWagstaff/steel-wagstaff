Afterwards, he tried again and again
to put into words the bliss of those moments
when he looked at the snow on the garage roof
shining in the moonlight.
It remained a reminder of the constant beauty about him
at which he had merely to gaze–
with sufficient perception.                    — Charles Reznikoff
