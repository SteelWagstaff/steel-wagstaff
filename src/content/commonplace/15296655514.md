The existence of the text is a silent existence, silent until the moment in which a reader reads it. Only when the able eye makes contact with the markings on the tablet does the text come to active life. All writing depends on the generosity of the reader.                    — Alberto Manguel, <em>History of Reading</em>. To which I would only add that reading also involves (at times) voices, hearing, and fingers (think of the touch of the blind involved in the reading of Braille).
