They are, frankly, extremely bad poetry – and you will notice that sometime. They are also very reassuring. It is important to be capable of extremely bad poetry, people who are not do not move  It is important not to be caught in one’s own first graces, one’s own first gracefulness It’s important that what’s difficult should be difficult. Difficulty, the refusal of things to work out, forces one deeper, that is, deep enough  In time ‘Out there’ among atomic things, atomic events – a difficult poetry       Don’t rush                    — George Oppen writing to his teenage niece after she sent him some poems.
