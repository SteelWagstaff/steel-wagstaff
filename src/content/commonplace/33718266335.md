Notably, if there are two cats in the yard, it is appropriate to say, while playing the game, that the number of cats in the yard is two. But this is only a pretense kept up while playing the game: we aren’t really committing ourselves to there being such things as numbers.                    — Gideon Rosen & John P. Burgess, “Nominalism Reconsidered”
