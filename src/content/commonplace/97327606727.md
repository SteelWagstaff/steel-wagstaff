During seven centuries a lot of subject matter of no great present interest has been stuffed into German verse that is not very skilful. I can see no reason why a foreign writer should study it.                    — Ezra Pound, ABC of Reading. And then, just a few pages later, he can say something like this. But it’s true, I guess? The Germans are the worst.
