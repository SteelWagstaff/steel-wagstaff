… the meaning of Charles [Reznikoff]’s poetry to me is a small man walking about a city, his eyes and his ears alive in the city, who sees everything and this is the politics. The rest, the lesson for young poets, is a very simple one. It need only be perfect                    — George Oppen, 1976 interview on KPFA, Berkeley
