*Tu te coucheras sur la terre simple,  
De qui tenais-tu qu’elle t’appartînt?*

*Du ciel inchangé l’errante lumière  
Recommencera l’éternel matin.*

*Tu croiras renaître aux heures profondes  
Du feu renoncé, du feu mal éteint.*

*Mais l’ange veindra de ses mains de cendre  
Étouffer l’ardeur qui n’a pas de fin.*

*

You lie down on the simple ground,  
Who ever told you it belonged to you?

The wandering light of the unchanged sky  
Will recommence the eternal morning.

You will believe things reborn in the deep hours  
of the renounced, poorly extinguished fire.

But the angel will come with his hands of ash  
To smother the ardor that has no end.

– Yves Bonnefoy, from *Hier Régnant Désert* [Yesterday’s Empty Kingdom] (1959). Bonnefoy died on July 1, 2016. He was 93 years old.
