> [untitled], Yves Bonnefoy Tu te coucheras sur la terre simple,De qui tenais-tu qu’elle t’appartînt?Du ciel inchangé l’errante lumièreRecommencera l’éternel matin.Tu croiras renaître aux heures profondesDu feu renoncé, du feu mal éteint.Mais l’ange veindra de ses mains de cendreÉtouffer l’ardeur qui n’a pas de fin.*You lie down on the simple ground,Who ever told you it belonged to you?The wandering light of the unchanged skyWill recommence the eternal morning.You will believe things reborn in the deep hoursof the renounced, poorly extinguished fire.But the angel will come with his hands of ashTo smother the ardor that has no end.– Yves Bonnefoy, from Hier Régnant Désert [Yesterday’s Empty Kingdom] (1959). Bonnefoy died on July 1, 2016. He was 93 years old.
