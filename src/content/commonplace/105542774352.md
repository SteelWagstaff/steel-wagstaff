Been rediscovering my sixteen year old self in the pictures I remember at the National museum and the Tate gallery. It is a strange experience to recognize what a very small and stubborn core is oneself. It is the same pictures that are meaningful to us. It particular, we had quite an affair with Turner.                    — George Oppen writing to his daughter, Linda, 26 May 1961.
