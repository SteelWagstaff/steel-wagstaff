> <p>An old friend of my father&rsquo;s once said to me when I was one day raving against a flagrant miscarriage of justice in our local courts, &ldquo;Willie, what do you think you can get in a court of law?&rdquo;</p> <p>&ldquo;Why the least you can ask for,&rdquo; I said, &ldquo;is common justice.&rdquo;</p> <p>&ldquo;Oh, no,&rdquo; he replied, &ldquo;you won&rsquo;t get justice, that&rsquo;s impossible. All you&rsquo;ll get is the best that is available in your locality.&rdquo;</p>

— William Carlos Williams' <em>Autobiography</em>
