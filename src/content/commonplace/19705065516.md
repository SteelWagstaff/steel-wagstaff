An old friend of my father’s once said to me when I was one day raving against a flagrant miscarriage of justice in our local courts, “Willie, what do you think you can get in a court of law?”

“Why the least you can ask for,” I said, “is common justice.”

“Oh, no,” he replied, “you won’t get justice, that’s impossible. All you’ll get is the best that is available in your locality.”                    — William Carlos Williams' Autobiography
