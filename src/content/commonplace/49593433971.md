A certain man once lost a diamond cuff-link in the wide blue sea, and twenty years later, on the exact day, a Friday apparently, he was eating a large fish - but there was no diamond inside. That’s what I like about coincidence.                    

— <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Nabokov">Vladimir Nabokov</a> was a Russian-American novelist (1899-1977), _<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laughter_in_the_Dark_(novel)">Laughter in the Dark</a>_, Bobbs-Merrill Company, 1932.
