I’ve wondered lately whether intelligence and reading are not handicaps, very poor substitutes for a sharp eye or an attentive ear. All my generation, which isn’t so much older than yours, were too bookish, except WCW. Yet I’m disgusted by the crowd of writers who have read only Time or some less durable antagonist of Time. Perhaps a poet’s training should be in biology or physics. Too many of us live in echo chambers.                    — Basil Bunting, in a January 1973 letter to George Oppen.
