> I&rsquo;ve wondered lately whether intelligence and reading are not handicaps, very poor substitutes for a sharp eye or an attentive ear. All my generation, which isn&rsquo;t so much older than yours, were too bookish, except WCW. Yet I&rsquo;m disgusted by the crowd of writers who have read only Time or some less durable antagonist of Time. Perhaps a poet&rsquo;s training should be in biology or physics. Too many of us live in echo chambers.

— Basil Bunting, in a January 1973 letter to George Oppen.
