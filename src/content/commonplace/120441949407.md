Heraldry has had an even stronger influence on sports: badges, flags and banners, sports shirts, the scarves and streamers waved by the fans, all these are heraldic … For example, few people know that the colours of the two prestigious Milan football clubs (Inter and AC Milan) were the emblematic tinctures of two districts of that city in the 16th century.
