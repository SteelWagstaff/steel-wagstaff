An examination of the incidence of the various tincture, for example, reveals a surprising emphasis on the colour blue throughout the West between the 13th and the 18th centuries; in arms, as in clothing and every social code, over the centuries blue was chosen in preference to red, the primordial colour. Indeed, even today it is by far the preferred colour of Europeans, whereas the ROmans considered it ‘barbaric’ and at the time of Charlemagne no king or count wore blue.
