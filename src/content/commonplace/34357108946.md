> From a biography of Jane Carlyle Uncle: Are you ready to go to church? Jane: No, I have no thought of it. Uncle: And why not? (Crescendo) Jane: Because your minister is a ranting jackass, that cracks the drums of one's ears. Uncle: And what has that to do with going to a place of worship? Jane: Nothing whatever; but it has a great deal to do with staying from a place that is not of worship.
