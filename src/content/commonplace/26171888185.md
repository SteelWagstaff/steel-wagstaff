There are things for each of us around which meaning gathers . . The mission is to hold them, to be able to keep them in his mind, to try again and again to find the word, the syntax, the cadence of unfolding – I don’t mean to promise redemption of course. A matter of being able to say what one is and where one is. And what matters.                    — George Oppen, letter to Frederic Will [written after July 22, 1967].
