O a man as treats his ass  
as a ass  
ought to be treated  
& it’s just the same if he has a pony  
can’t be too perticler  
if I had my way I’d ‘act a law  
making people particler  
about ‘osses & asses’ shoes  
if your boot pinches you sir  
you can sing out to your bootmaker  
but a ass can’t blow up a farrier
