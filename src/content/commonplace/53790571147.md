I saw this brief statement from General David Morrison (currently Australia’s Chief of Army) about allegations of disgraceful sexual exploitation by Australian servicemen a little over a week ago and was impressed. Plain speaking, clear message, right tone. I believe that we need similar messengers and the will to transform abusive, sexist environments in our own military (and broader) culture.
