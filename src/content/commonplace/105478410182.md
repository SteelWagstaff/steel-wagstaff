Let me add a word or two about George and Mary … I had a very good time there [the Poetry Festival of the Thomas Jefferson College near Grand Rapids, Michigan] and their presence certainly added to my pleasure. George got a standing ovation at the end of his reading and what pleased me also was to see how well he danced with a marvelous dancer in the lobby of our dorm.                    — Charles Reznikoff to June Oppen Degnan, June 29, 1973
