> During the study period, the numbers showed a big spike between 2005 and 2008, with five cases in the last five months of 2005, eight in 2007 — and 25 in the first 11 months of 2008. Victims suffered massive facial trauma, brain injuries and serious fractures of clavicles, ribs ankles and arms. No one died. Most simply fell off the devices, typically when they encountered an immobile object such as a bench, tree or pole, McKay said. Seventy percent of those hurt were visitors to the D.C. area, including some who tried Segways as part of guided sightseeing tours, the study found.
