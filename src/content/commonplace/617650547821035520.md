> ‪“To the Tune of ‘Dream Song’”‬ ‪I’ll never forget sunset at Brook Pavilion—‬‪drunk with beauty, we lost our way.‬‪When the ecstasy faded, we turned our boat home,‬‪but it was late and we strayed into a place deep with lotus flowers‬‪and rowed hard, so hard‬‪the whole shore erupted with herons and gulls.‬‪— Li Qingzhao (1084- c. 1155/1156)‬, translated from the Chinese by Tony Barnstone and Chou Ping
