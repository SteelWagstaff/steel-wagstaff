Bubbles  
and sparks  
–when consulted–  
say  
that they would like to stay longer.

The hardest crystals  
and oldest metals  
declare the same:

there would be no two opinions  
on this subject.

–Time.  
–More time.  
–A little more time.

from The Invisible Bridge: Selected Poems of Circe Maia, translated by Jesse Lee Kercheval  

c.f. Tom Waits’ “It’s the same with men as with horses and dogs / nothing wants to die”
