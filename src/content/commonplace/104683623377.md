> The Prairie & The City [Oppen] L.S. Dembo: What would the prairie have to do with the city, then? George Oppen: It's there. It's just there, under the pavement. It is that ground. L.S. Dembo: I'm not sure I follow you. George Oppen: I just meant, my eye sees the plain land. It's just land after all, even though they've paved this thing and created all this complication on it.
