You have read about the recent phenomenon in modern art. … I don’t know what the value of modern art is. I mean either way. But Mr. Khrushchev visited such a place, and Mr. Khrushchev decided that it looked as if this painting were painted by the tail of a jackass. My comment is, he should know.                    — Richard Feynman, The Meaning of It All.
