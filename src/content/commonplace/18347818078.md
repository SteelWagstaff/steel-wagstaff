> The world is very large, I think, and I certainly can&rsquo;t testify to the whole of it. I can only testify to my own feelings; I can only say what I saw and heard, and I try to say it as well as I can. And if your conclusion is that what I saw and heard makes you feel the way I did, then the poem is successful.

— Charles Reznikoff, in response to L.S. Dembo&rsquo;s question: &ldquo;Well, exactly what are you testifying to as a poet-the world or your own feelings or both?&rdquo;
