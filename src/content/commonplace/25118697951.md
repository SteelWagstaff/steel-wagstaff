Anita
was thinking – after our conversation:
the phrase ‘black is beautiful’ was a life-saving slogan. A blaze of light, I think it lit so many lives
so:      it was poetry
The man, woman who said that knew it had the force of poetry
To repeat it is 'political poetry.’ That is, to repeat it is to recite poetry
Recitiing poetry and writing poetry are not the same thing    (no objection to reciting poetry of course)
(( Whitman celebrated America       Because he had seen it, it came to him
I hope that by the year tho-thousand   black is beautiful will be something in the nature of Fourth of July oratory.     Recitation.
(nice visit)
George
