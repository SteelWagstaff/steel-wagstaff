> <p>Anita<br/> was thinking &ndash; after our conversation:<br/> the phrase &lsquo;black is beautiful&rsquo; was a life-saving slogan. A blaze of light, I think it lit so many lives<br/> so: it was poetry<br/> The man, woman who said that knew it had the force of poetry<br/> To repeat it is 'political poetry.&rsquo; That is, to repeat it is to recite poetry<br/> Recitiing poetry and writing poetry are not the same thing (no objection to reciting poetry of course)<br/> (( Whitman celebrated America Because he had seen it, it came to him<br/> I hope that by the year tho-thousand black is beautiful will be something in the nature of Fourth of July oratory. Recitation.<br/> (nice visit)<br/> George</p> <p><br/> (re- cite)</p>

— George Oppen, letter to Anita Barrows, probably October 1974
