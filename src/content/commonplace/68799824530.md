> Rather than a horizontal community of scholars, or even a pyramid with reasonable steps of rank, the American university has adopted its own harsh class structure: the mass of the contingent (and other workers) struggling at the bottom, tenure-stream professors in the middle class speaking for the university&rsquo;s intellectual values and productions, and superstar faculty and administrators in the upper class setting its direction and taking the greatest rewards.

— Jeffrey J. Williams, &ldquo;<a href="https:::::://chronicle.com/article/The-Great-Stratification/143285/">The Great Stratification</a>&rdquo;
