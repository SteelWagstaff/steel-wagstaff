> <p>We must talk now. I am no longer sure of the words,<br/> The clockwork of the world. What is inexplicable</p> <p>Is the &lsquo;preponderance of objects.&rsquo; The sky lights<br/> Daily with that predominance</p> <p>And we have become the present.</p>

— from George Oppen&rsquo;s &ldquo;<a href="https::::::::::://www.poetryfoundation.org/poem/238466">Leviathan</a>&rdquo; in _The Materials_
