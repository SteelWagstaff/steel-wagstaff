**Mary Oppen:**
With such excitement-she [Buddhadeva Bose's daughter, Ruby] must have been very homesick, to have told us. Oh, and Ginsberg had come to see them and had stayed with them.

**David McAleavey:**
In India, you mean? . . .

**MO:**
Yes. Her mother is a writer of movies, of those millions of movies that--she earns the family income.

**GO:**
Invited Ginsberg to a big party, and he had said, "I'll come provided I--"

**MO:**
"Can be naked."

**GO:**
"Can be naked," something.

**MO:**
And her mother-

**GO:**
Said-

**MO:**
Thought it over-

**GO:**
She said, "Well, this is a poor country. Many people don't have clothes. Nobody'll mind." [MO laughs] Nobody did mind.
