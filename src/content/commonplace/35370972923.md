> Still, I think our old, good woman Earth, though torn by political conflict, is the only world we have and we&rsquo;re all her tenants depending in one way or another on each other. &hellip; I want to be a mail boat for everyone divided by the ice of estrangement, a craft before the coming of large navigation, moving through the drifting ice with letters and parcels.
