> <p>There are billboards up advertising Perrier, which is more or less the Tehuacan water of France, reading: </p> <p>Pshitt!<br/> C'est la qualité de Perrier</p> <p>Can&rsquo;t explain it. Either they mean it, or Pshitt to the French ear suggests the tempting sound of bubble-water.</p>

— George Oppen, 1961 letter to his daughter Linda, written in Paris.
