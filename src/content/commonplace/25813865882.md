There are billboards up advertising Perrier, which is more or less the Tehuacan water of France, reading: 

Pshitt!
C'est la qualité de Perrier

Can’t explain it. Either they mean it, or Pshitt to the French ear suggests the tempting sound of bubble-water.                    — George Oppen, 1961 letter to his daughter Linda, written in Paris.
