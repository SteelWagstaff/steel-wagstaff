Sempre compassionare fu tempo perduto, / l'esistenza è tremenda e non muta per questo, / meglio stringere i denti e tacere.                    — Compassion was always a waste of time / existence is terrible and compassion will not change it, / it’s better to grit one’s teeth and keep silent.
Cesare Pavese, from “Donne perdute" ("Fallen Women”)
