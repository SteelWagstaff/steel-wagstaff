> Magnificent Peak By its own nature it towers above the tangle of riversDon’t say it’s a lot of dirt piled highWithout end the mist of dawn the evening cloud draw their shadows across itFrom the four directions you can look up and see it green and steep and wild.– Musō Soseki (1275-1351), translated from the Japanese by W. S. Merwin
