  <blockquote>
  <p>Writer, critic, and activist <a href=\"http://www.poets.org/poet.php/prmPID/445\">Amiri Baraka</a> died on January 9, 2014, and we remember him by taking a look at his little magazine, <em>Yugen</em>, “a new consciousness in arts and letters,” which he published with Hettie Cohen from 1958-1961. Here in the Little Magazine Collection we have the full run (<a href=\"http://search.library.wisc.edu/catalog/ocm04178897\">issues 1-7</a>).</p>
  <p>The Oxford Critical and Cultural History of Modernist Magazines, Vol. II, has a profile on Yugen, noting its “Beat sensibility,” its “air of radicalism,” and its “propensity to eschew rational logic for surreal juxtaposition,” also praising the magazine for reflecting cross-semination of poets from various regions and schools. In its pages are Ginsberg and Di Prima, Corso and Burroughs, Snyder, Creeley, Corso, and more, and includes, in issues 4-7, a dispute between Creeley and Corso over whether Black Mountain “over-value[d] theory”. (Read the full <a href=\"http://books.google.com/books?id=QEta9eqdG2IC&amp;pg=PA1021&amp;lpg=PA1021&amp;dq=amiri+baraka+yugen+magazine&amp;source=bl&amp;ots=46_8Te7rm0&amp;sig=jVRXsBZX_tiXYKziNYZ6ODM2Hrk&amp;hl=en&amp;sa=X&amp;ei=dmHVUsfnMeHhyQHG-IGwDA&amp;ved=0CG8Q6AEwCQ#v=onepage&amp;q=amiri%20baraka%20yugen%20magazine&amp;f=false\">profile</a> of <em>Yugen</em> for more info.)</p>
  <p>Yugen ceased publication in 1961 but that same year Baraka began to publish <em>The Floating Bear, </em>a poetry newsletter,with Di Prima, and would for the next ten years. We have <a href=\"http://search.library.wisc.edu/catalog/ocm01569373\">those</a> in the Little Magazine Collection, too (nos. 1-38), and Greenwich Village History has an <a href=\"http://gvh.aphdigital.org/collections/show/20\">online archive</a> of the newsletter that is worth checking out.</p>
  <p>We end with some of his own words: “There is no depth to education without art.” (<em>Digging: The Afro-American Soul of American Classical Music</em>, 2009).</p>
  </blockquote>
