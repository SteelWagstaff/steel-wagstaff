And speech is bright  
it needs no candle

as two people converse  
silence listens  
and can then be heard

words also shine  
bright as fire

sojourners here  
we name in all the languages of earth

the words for rock  
to make the rock       be rock

though we name  
granite      basalt       pebble      stone

the rock is held unaltered in the mind  
as rock in earth remains itself

–from *Poems and Transpositions *(1980)
