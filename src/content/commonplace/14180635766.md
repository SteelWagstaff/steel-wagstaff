> Our artificial Science has relation to the forms of Nature, but yet that which is most important in Nature &ndash; Life &ndash; is above our science. The Astronomer vainly asserts the perfection of his Science because he is capable of determining the motions of 7 planets and 22 satellites; but comets &amp; meteors which even move in our system are above his reach, &amp; even this solar system is a speck in the immensity of space &amp; suns and worlds are above our reach. Our Science refers to the globe only, &amp; in this there is an endless field for investigation: the interior is unknown; the causes of Volcanoes. We have just learnt some truths with respect to the surface: but there is an immensity beneath us. &ndash; Geology in every sense of the word is a superficial science.

— March 1814 entry in Humphry Davy&rsquo;s journal, qtd in Harold Hartley&rsquo;s biography.
