To carry on the feelings of childhood into the powers of manhood; to combine the child’s sense of wonder and novelty with the appearances which every day for perhaps forty years had rendered familiar … this is the character and privilege of genius, and one of the marks which distinguish genius from talent.                    — Samuel Taylor Coleridge, Biographia Literaria
