Your hair has turned white  
While your heart stayed  
Knotted against me.  
I shall never  
Loosen it now.

– Hitomaro

Will I cease to be,  
Or will I remember  
Beyond the world,  
Our last meeting together?

– Lady Izumi Shikibu

The flowers whirl away  
In the wind like snow.  
The thing that falls away  
Is myself.

– The Prime Minister Kintsune

As certain as color  
Passes from the petal,  
Irrevocable as flesh,  
The gazing eyes falls through the world.

– The Poetess Ono No Komachi

The the dawn, although I know  
It will grow dark again,  
How I hate the coming day.

– Fujiwara No Michinobu

In the mountain village  
The wind rustles the leaves.  
Deep in the night, the deer  
Cry out beyond the edge of dreams.

– Minamoto No Morotada

If only the world  
Would always remain this way,  
Some fishermen  
Drawing a little rowboat  
Up the river bank.

– The Shogun Minamoto No Sanetomo

In all the world  
There is no way whatever.  
The stag cries even  
In the most remote mountain.

– The Priest Fujiwara No Toshinari

No, the human heart  
Is unknowable.  
But in my birthplace  
The flowers still smell  
The same as always.

– Tsurayuki

The wind rustles the bamboos  
By my window in the dusk.

– Yakamochi

A bilnd child  
Guided by his mother,  
Admires the cherry blossoms.

– Kikaku

No one spoke,  
The host, the guest,  
The white chrysanthemums.

– Ryota
