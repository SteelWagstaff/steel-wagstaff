He travels on, and in his face, his step, / His gait, is one expression; every limb, / His look and bending figure, all bespeak / A man who does not move with pain, but moves / With thought–                    — Wordsworth, from “Old Man Travelling; Animal Tranquility and Decay, a Sketch”
