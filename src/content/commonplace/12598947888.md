at my aunt’s funeral last week, her family released white doves at the graveside to symbolize the parts of them that are moving on with her spirit. to stand with my pillars in celebration of a glorious life, to watch those fragile solitary birds circle into a flock in the snapcold sky, to feel the flap of desire to go where they’re going but know that i’m ground-bound–sometimes i’m stopped stilled by the texture of experience, how beautiful and complicated and simple and all it is.                    — Catherine Curtis
