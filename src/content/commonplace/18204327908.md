> It's Not a Contest For the past several weeks, every time I’ve passed the northwest corner of the Humanities Building on the UW-Madison campus, I’ve seen a message scrawled on the pale sandstone in green chalk: “China #1 Human Rights Offender in the World.” Today, I saw that some one had rubbed out the word offender, but left the rest of the message intact. I’m not sure what particular offense or offenses motivated the original message, but I’ve been tempted several times to take my own piece of chalk (if I ever remembered to go and buy one for the purpose) and write beneath the first message my own response: “It’s not a contest.”
