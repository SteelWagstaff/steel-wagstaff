I see the dead / in naked majesty, consumed with longing / for what we in the common street have by heart; / the leaf’s errant fall, a child’s cry. Delicate, / brutal, impure, pure–the world, the world / tears them apart.                    — Daniel Berrigan, from “Travelers” in No One Walks Waters.
