> Thank You, My Fate Great humility fill me,great purity fills me,I make love with my dearas if I made love dyingas if I made love praying,tears pourover my arms and his arms.I don’t know whether this is joyor sadness, I don’t understandwhat I feel, I’m crying,I’m crying, it’s humilityas if I were dead,gratitude, I thank you, my fate,I’m unworthy, how beautifulmy life.– Anna Swir (1909 - 1984), translated from the Polish by Czesław Miłosz and Leonard Nathan. I’m so grateful to Ewa Miernowska for recommending Swir’s poetry to me. It’s excellent.
