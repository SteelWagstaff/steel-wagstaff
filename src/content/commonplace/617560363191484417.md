Great humility fill me,  
great purity fills me,  
I make love with my dear  
as if I made love dying  
as if I made love praying,  
tears pour  
over my arms and his arms.  
I don’t know whether this is joy  
or sadness, I don’t understand  
what I feel, I’m crying,  
I’m crying, it’s humility  
as if I were dead,  
gratitude, I thank you, my fate,  
I’m unworthy, how beautiful  
my life.

– Anna Swir (1909 - 1984), translated from the Polish by Czesław Miłosz and Leonard Nathan. I’m so grateful to Ewa Miernowska for recommending Swir’s poetry to me. It’s excellent.
