> October 15 entry from Bob Arnold’s This Romance: [on a rooster] His sound is breath for life. As a friend visiting us said, when looking at Jack the malamute, who is deaf and slower but commonly magnificent in the beauty of his face, how would anyone think anybody could be–ugly as an animal–when animals, their faces, are beautiful and full-faced and harbor no disguise. It is what you life in your hands, and the eyes blink a return gaze into yours. Apples, firewood, tools, faces, to be forever thankful for that all of everything held in two hands.
