The first match in Austria took place on November 15, 1894 between the Vienna Cricket Club and gardeners from Baron Rothschild’s estate, but local interest was so great that by 1911 the cricket club had become Wiener Amateure.                    — Jonathan Wilson’s outstanding Inverting the Pyramid.
