After a few minutes the spectacle has given way to just another game [Super Bowl XXIX], which is unbelievably boring, so Natalie and I go play in a nearby park. Climbing a metal-chain ladder, she knows exactly how high she can climb and still feel comfortable, at what exact point she’s scared. Being a parent is, to me, an endless lesson in, among other things, Mind Knows Nothing–Body Knows Everything.                    — David Shields, Black Planet
