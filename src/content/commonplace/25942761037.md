The simplest  
Words say the grass blade  
Hides the blaze  
Of a sun  
To throw a shadow  
In which the bugs crawl  
At the roots of the grass;

Father, father  
Of fatherhood  
Who haunts me, shivering  
Man most naked  
Of us all, O father

           watch  
At the roots  
Of the grass the creating  
*Now*         that tremendous  
plunge 

–George Oppen. Poem published in his 1965 collection *This in Which*
