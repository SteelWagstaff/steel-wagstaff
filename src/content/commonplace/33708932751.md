> <p>Nothing<br/> substance utters or time<br/> stills or restrains<br/> joins the design and the</p> <p>supple measure deftly<br/> as thought’s intricate polyphonic<br/> score dovetails with the tread<br/> sensuous things<br/> keep in our consciousness.</p> <p>Celebrate man’s craft<br/> and the word spoken in shapeless night, the<br/> sharp tool paring away<br/> waste and the forms<br/> cut out of mystery!</p>

— First three stanzas of Basil Bunting&rsquo;s poem &ldquo;The Word,&rdquo; published in _Poetry, _<a href="http://www.poetryfoundation.org/poetrymagazine/browse/37/5#/20577914/0">February 1931</a> (the &ldquo;Objectivist&rdquo; issue).
