They are the same everywhere. <br/>
This one driving <br/>
his cart along the Danube, <br/>
why should he give up <br/>
his good sheep <br/>
and his open fields<br/>
 and the sight of goats <br/>
on the Carpathians <br/>
for a strange war <br/>
in a strange land?                    — Carl Rakosi, “Country People Never Learn”
