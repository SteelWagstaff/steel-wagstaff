They are the same everywhere. 
This one driving 
his cart along the Danube, 
why should he give up 
his good sheep 
and his open fields
 and the sight of goats 
on the Carpathians 
for a strange war 
in a strange land?                    — Carl Rakosi, “Country People Never Learn”
