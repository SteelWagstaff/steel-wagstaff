The drunken man   
On an old pier   
In the Hudson River

Tightening his throat, thrust his chin   
Forward and the light   
Caught his face   
His eyes still blind with drink

Said, to my wife   
And to me —   
He must have been saying

Again —

Good bye Momma   
Good bye Poppa

On an old pier
