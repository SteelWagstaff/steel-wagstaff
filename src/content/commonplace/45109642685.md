> As for who is a Jewish poet, there is one fundamental test: humor. If not every funny, then not Jewish. Pound is probably not Jewish. Zukofsky maybe. Oppen may have a problem.

— Hank Lazer, &ldquo;Who or What is a Jewish American Poet, with Specific Reference to David Antin, Charles Bernstein, Rachel Blau DuPlessis, and Jerome Rothenberg&rdquo; in _Radical Poetics and Secular Jewish Culture_.
