> A god could have brought the body of Hector back,<br/> just as Phoebus kept it from decay,<br/> but Priam had to get it himself;<br/> so Pallas could have killed Hector easily,<br/> but Achilles had to do it.<br/> She would hand him his spear when he missed<br/> but he had to throw it again

— Charles Reznikoff, &ldquo;Lesson in Homer&rdquo; from _By the Well of Living and Seeing _(1969)
