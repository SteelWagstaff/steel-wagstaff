“By the way, [Ossip] Zadkine, a past Time Magazine tells me, has a statue in Rotterdam called The Devastated City – has a hold where the heart of the man – figure – should be. It looks unfinished but Zadkine said “it a bird builds a nest in the hole, the statue will then be considered finished.” – Lorine Niedecker to Louis Zukofsky, 13 December, 1963
