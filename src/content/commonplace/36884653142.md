> George Oppen on culture If, in a poor country, … someone suggested: Let us hold a raffle, and thru a system of tokens … give to a few individuals the right to use the labor of hundreds or even thousands so that at least someone among us will be able really to taste life, to see the world, perhaps to report on it, to enjoy love without stint, to read and to think, and to exhaust the possibilities of adventure and of pleasure—if someone suggested that in a hopeless country, I think I would be tempted to agree. But actually, those who are able to claim the product of others’ labor see nothing, learn nothing, feel nothing, are pre-occupied almost continually with the attempt to avoid discomfort, at which somehow they fail.They appear to read little and to think not at all, love is no more prevalent among them than among the poorestThe princess suffers agonies because there is a lump in the bed; the millionaireness nearly dies because the plane was not adequately heated.
