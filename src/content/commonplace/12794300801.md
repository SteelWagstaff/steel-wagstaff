I don’t think he was finally a literary person, not in the usual sense at least. He loved books but he questioned the decor of the literary habit. Too pious, too pretentious. We both believed in the necessary bedrock of a common world. It’s finally hard to think of anyone more decent; kind really, than Fred was. It seems now altogether stupid even to try.                    — Robert Creeley, on Fred (Frederick) Eckman
