> The critic is not the one who debunks, but the one who assembles. The critic is not the one who lifts the rugs from under the feet of the naive believers, but the one who offers the participants arenas in which to gather. The critic is not the one who alternates haphazardly between antifetishism and positivism &hellip; but the one for whom, if something is constructed, then it means it is fragile and thus in great need of care and caution.

— Bruno Latour, &ldquo;<a href="http://mendota.english.wisc.edu/~clc/Latour.pdf">Why Has Critique Run Out of Steam? From Matters of Fact to Matters of Concern</a>&rdquo;
