I come as a guest  
entering my own life

and the tree that leans    lends  
me     its strength

what my mothers said,  
the dreams  
I disappear into in sleep

in safety    I dream danger  
I    open my eyes  
startled that I am safe

we walk in autumn    stubble  
the field not ours  
small houses     unfurnished     empty  
we enter  
and it is our home
