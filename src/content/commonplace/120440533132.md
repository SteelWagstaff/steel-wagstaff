Blazon arranges its six tinctures into two main groups – metals and colours. (There is also a third group, furs: Ermine and Vair.) It places Argent [silver/white] and Or [gold/yellow] in the first, Gules [red], Sable [black], Azure [blue], and Vert [green] in the second. The basic rule is that a colour may not be placed on a colour or a metal on a metal. … These fundamental rules have existed since the origin of arms and have almost always been respected – it is rare to find one per cent of infringements in a given body of arms.
