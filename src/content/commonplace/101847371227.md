> When the heart is drained of desire<br/> the revenue&rsquo;s nothing but rubbish.<br/> If a man will sell his brother for rubbish<br/> they are right to deny whatever he claims by birth.

— From Afaridun&rsquo;s response to Salm &amp; Tur in Ferdowsi's <em>&ldquo;Faridun&rsquo;s Sons&rdquo; </em>[a section of the <em>Shahnameh</em>], translated by Basil Bunting. From <em><a href="http://www.floodeditions.com/bunting-persia" target="_blank">Bunting&rsquo;s Persia</a></em>.
