When the heart is drained of desire
the revenue’s nothing but rubbish.
If a man will sell his brother for rubbish
they are right to deny whatever he claims by birth.                    — From Afaridun’s response to Salm & Tur in Ferdowsi's “Faridun’s Sons” [a section of the Shahnameh], translated by Basil Bunting. From Bunting’s Persia.
