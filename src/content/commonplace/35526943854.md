The revolution  
is accomplished  
noble has been  
changed to no bull

After that  
has sickered down  
slumming will  
be done on Park Ave.

Or as Chief  
One Horn said to  
the constipated  
prospector:

You big fool!  
and with his knife  
gashed a balsam  
standing nearby

Gathering the  
gum that oozed out  
in a tin spoon  
it did the trick

You can do lots  
if you know  
what’s around you  
No bull
