> I wonder what the mind will be capable of doing someday without danger to the entire body? – it’s a terrible isolation though the most beautiful and thrilling thing that can happen to us. But it won’t ever happen – and live. It’s tied to the body and the body is the bigger thing, probably always will be. It’s only a small specialized thing – a benign cancer! – an aeronautical appendage, something in space to explore well enough tied to the world of the body to suffer.

— Lorine Niedecker, letter to Louis Zukofsky, Aug. 20, 1952.
