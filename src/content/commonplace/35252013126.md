> <p>Litzel will appear on this fall’s ballot in Iowa as an independent candidate for president. &hellip; Litzel, self-described as “very conservative,” hadn’t even earned his own support this fall until the past few days. He said he had been considering voting for Romney, but, “after this deal, I’ll go with myself.”</p> <p>Litzel, who will only appear on the ballot in Iowa, has previously run, unsuccessfully, for the Iowa Legislature, Congress and county supervisor, always as an independent. &hellip; His brother, Jim Litzel, of Harbor City, Calif., is his running mate.</p> <p>“Four years from now we’ve got big plans, but I won’t talk about that yet,” Litzel said.</p>
