> Miss Hayes liked to tell stories about her own unworldliness. There was the time, for instance, when her husband took her to call on the writer Gertrude Stein and Miss Stein&rsquo;s friend Alice B. Toklas in their Paris home. Although the walls were decorated with fine paintings, Miss Hayes later recalled, &ldquo;Nothing impressed me more than Alice B. Toklas&rsquo;s mustache.&rdquo;
