This week Dave and Steel are joined once again by Dave's old neighbor Matt Brown. Together the trio discusses Matt's vinyl collection, the best and worst American sports teams owners in recent memory, give their recommendations for book of the year, play great music by Pavement, Jonathan Candido, Andrew W.K., The Baseball Project, and Barrett Strong, and share book wisdom from Don DeLillo's first novel, Americana.

### BASEBALL

#### BAD

- [Frank McCourt](http://www.slate.com/articles/sports/sports_nut/2011/04/hit_the_road_frank.html) \[Dodgers\]

### **FOOTBALL**

#### BAD

- [Mark Davis](http://espn.go.com/nfl/story/_/id/13735322/are-mark-davis-raiders-leaving-oakland) and [his amazing hair cut](http://nextimpulsesports.com/2015/05/19/raiders-owner-mark-davis-continues-to-rock-the-best-haircut-in-the-nfl/). He co-owns the Oakland Raiders with his mother.
- Dan Snyder \[Washington Redskins\]. I may not be an expert in this but the name seems racist.
- [Jim Irsay](http://www.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/colts/2014/03/27/jim-irsay-arrest-reports-tell-of-cash-pills-erratic-driving/6949251/) \[Colts\] and his '[shadow life](http://espn.go.com/nfl/story/_/id/11705479/investigations-reveal-shadow-life-indianapolis-colts-owner-jim-irsay)'.

#### GOOD

- [Green Bay Packers, Inc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Bay_Packers,_Inc.)., the only community owned team in major American pro sports.

### **BASKETBALL**

#### BAD

- Donald Sterling \[LA Clippers\]. This guy’s [whole life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Sterling). Holy crap.

#### GOOD

- Steve Ballmer \[LA Clippers\]. Amazing everything. [THIS COMPANY YAAAAAAAAAAAASSSS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvsboPUjrGc)!
- Jim Dolan \[NY Knicks\]. [Amazing musician](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sm2v9qCtLc0), bizarre friendship with Isiah Thomas
- Mikhail Prokhorov \[Brooklyn Nets\]. Just read [this](http://www.newrepublic.com/article/world/98577/crazy-prokhorov-facts). Yikes.
- Ozzie and Daniel Silna \[Spirits of St. Louis\]. These brothers and former ABA team owners worked out a deal guaranteeing them a fixed share of TV money _in perpetuity_ for giving up their team. [Best legal deal of all time](http://www.nytimes.com/2014/01/07/sports/basketball/payout-may-come-for-an-aba-team-that-is-long-gone.html)?
