In the first episode of our _Off the Chain_ podcast, we discuss the meaning of 'Off the Chain,' the [_Planet Hulk_](https://marvel.wikia.com/Planet_Hulk "Planet Hulk") storyline from Marvel's [_The Incredible Hulk_](https://marvel.com/universe/Planet_Hulk) series, the Liam Neeson film [_Non-Stop_](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jiHDJ19A3dk "Non-Stop [2014]"), and the music of [Damien Jurado](https://damienjurado.com "Damien Jurado").

We're also including a Damien Jurado sampler playlist with some of Steel's favorite songs, in case you want a taste of his music. The Spotify playlist features music from most of his albums, presented in rough chronological order. You can listen to that below:
