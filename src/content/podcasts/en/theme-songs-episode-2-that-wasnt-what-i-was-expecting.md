On this week's episode Dave & Steel are joined by their old friend Spencer to play music from bands with misleading names, including Los Campesinos!, The Final Solution, Yo La Tengo, Dr. Dog, Chinese Man, and St. Paul & The Broken Bones. https://open.spotify.com/user/steelwagstaff/playlist/6pZYgbqOPR6uQ4lYCZ1rob Featured image: The faces of the three folks calling themselves \"Yo La Tengo.\" That feeling you're experiencing? That's called cognitive dissonance.
