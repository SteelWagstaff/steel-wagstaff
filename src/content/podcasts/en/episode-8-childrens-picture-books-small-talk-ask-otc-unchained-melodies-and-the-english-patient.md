This week Dave and Steel are joined by their old friend [Amy](http://clubnarwhal.blogspot.com/p/about.html) to discuss children's picture books, cave dwellings, pigeon-based Ponzi schemes, a once in a lifetime chance to own Hell, frog burgers, answer pressing questions from longtime listeners, share some of their favorite recent music, and drop some beauty from Michael Ondaatje.

## Links

### Children's Picture Books

#### Dave's recommendations

- [Peggy Rathmann](http://www.peggyrathmann.com/ "Peggy Rathmann"): She's my number one. We especially love _[Good Night, Gorilla](http://www.peggyrathmann.com/goodnightgorilla.html)_
- [Mo Willems](http://www.mowillems.com/ "Mo Willems"). Especially the [Elephant & Piggie series](http://www.pigeonpresents.com/books.aspx "Elephant & Piggie books").
- [Uri Shulevitz](http://us.macmillan.com/author/urishulevitz "Uri Shulevitz"). Especially his book _[Snow](http://us.macmillan.com/snow/urishulevitz "Uri Shulevitz' Snow")_

#### Amy's recommendations

- Harry G. Allard's _[Miss Nelson is Missing!](http://www.amazon.com/Miss-Nelson-Missing-Harry-Allard/dp/0395401461 "Miss Nelson is Missing!"), illustrated by James Marshall._
- [Dallas Clayton](http://www.veryawesomeworld.com/)'s _[An Awesome Book](http://www.amazon.com/dp/0062114689/ref=as_li_ss_til?tag=thepaperbriga-20&camp=0&creative=0&linkCode=as4&creativeASIN=0062114689&adid=1JBTWSNRRAJWETMFT5DM "Dallas Clayton's An Awesome Book")_.
- B.J. Novak's _[The Book with No Pictures](http://www.amazon.com/The-Book-Pictures-B-J-Novak/dp/0803741715 "B.J. Novak's The Book with No Pictures")_. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cREyQJO9EPs

#### Steel's recommendations

- Richard Scarry. Especially _[What do People Do All Day?](http://www.amazon.com/What-Do-People-All-Day/dp/0394818237 "What Do People Do All Day?")_ and _[Cars and Trucks and Things That Go](http://www.amazon.com/Richard-Scarrys-Cars-Trucks-Things/dp/0307157857/ref=asap_bc?ie=UTF8 "Cars and Trucks and Things That Go")_.
- Oscar Wilde's _[The Selfish Giant](http://www.amazon.com/Selfish-Giant-Michael-Neugebauer-book/dp/1558582932/kevinholtsber-20 "The Selfish Giant")_, with watercolor illustrations by Lizbeth Zwerger.
- Ingri and Edgar d'Aulaire's _[Book of Norse Myths](http://www.amazon.com/DAulaires-Norse-Myths-Ingri-dAulaire/dp/159017125X "Book of Norse Myths")_ and _[Book of Greek Myths](http://www.amazon.com/DAulaires-Greek-Myths-Ingri-dAulaire/dp/0440406943 "Book of Greek Myths")_.
