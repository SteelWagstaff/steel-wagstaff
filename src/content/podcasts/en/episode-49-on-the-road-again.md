This week Dave and Steel are joined by their friend Jordan--together the three of them spend about an hour talking about fatherhood, family, and survival as it relates to Cormac McCarthy's novel _The Road_, give updates on the life and times of American hero Vin Diesel, play great music by David Bowie, William Onyeabor, Interpol, and Dethklok, and share some wrenching book wisdom from the American poet George Oppen.
