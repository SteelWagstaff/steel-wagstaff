This week Dave and Steel bring back their old friend Amy (Scott) Anderson. Together the trio discuss epic meltdowns, some amazing things that have they've been dying to share with each other during Dave's paternity leave, talk (for a long time) about babies and parenting, share great music they've been digging recently by AURORA, the Strumbellas, Pinegrove, AdriAnne Lenker & Buck Meek, and Whitney, and Amy drops some impressive book wisdom from Paul Kalanithi's touching memoir _When Breath Becomes Air_.
