Looking for a 10 1/2 hour playlist? Then let me tell you about this one--it's a big collection of music I listened to and enjoyed in October of this year. Pretty eclectic, pretty varied stuff. Some records I finally got around to listening to in full and would recommend: Bon Iver's 22, A Million; Olafur Arnalds' Island Songs; Juana Molina's Un Dia and Wed 21; Margaret Glaspy's Emotions and Math; Merissa Nadler's Bury Your Name;Ciaran Lavery's Not Nearly Drunk; Conor Oberst's Upside Down Moutain; and Moses Sumney's EP Lamentations.
