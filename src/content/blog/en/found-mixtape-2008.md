For the next several weeks, I'm going to be posting a new mixtape each week. Most of these were CD mixes that I made at some point in the past half dozen or so years. I don't remember the occasion for this one, but I made it some time during the first year or two after I had moved to Wisconsin for grad school (maybe as part of a departmental mixtape exchange?). The only songs that didn't make it into the Spotify list were this live acoustic version of Beck performing Rosalie (between the Jayhawks and Turin Brakes) and the Travis b-side Only Molly Knows, sandwiched between Hayden and Feist.

Enjoy!
