Here's a 6+ hour playlist of songs I listened to and liked in April and May of 2014. Was on paternity leave for a big chunk of this time, getting to know a new being, and away from new music on my computer. It was so worth it. Favorites from this period included Clap! Clap!, Chinese Man, Sun Kil Moon's Benji record, Sam & Dave, Bowerbirds' The Clearing, Jack Rose, Richard Hawley's Standing at the Sky's End, Agnes Obel's Aventine album, Balmorhea's Stranger, Dorothy Love Coates, and Brass Bed's The Secret Will Keep You.
