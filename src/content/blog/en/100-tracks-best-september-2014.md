Here's a playlist of 100 songs I listened to and liked from the month of September. Some favorites from the month were Sarah Jaffe, The War on Drugs, Flume, Shad, Larry Gus's Years Not Living, Eef Barzelay's new Girls Come First EP, Snorri Helgason's I'm Going to Put My Name On Your Door,  Tamikrest's Chatma, Hammock's Oblivion Hymns, Bellows' Blue Breath record,  Josephine Foster's I'm a Dreamer album,  and some classic Don McLean (I listened to this with Cedar and thought of my dad, who introduced me to Don McLean).

Enjoy!
