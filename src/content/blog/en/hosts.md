Theme Songs is hosted by Dave & Steel, who previously made the podcast Off the Chain with Dave & Steel.
Dave Jenson
[caption id="attachment_739" align="aligncenter" width="790"] Dave as a kid with his siblings. He's the one furthest right.[/caption]

Dave lives in Prescott Valley, AZ with his wife and eight million children. Musically, the key to Dave's heart is syncopated rhythms, obscure, mumbled lyrics, and profoundly ugly lead singers.
Steel Wagstaff
[caption id="attachment_741" align="alignright" width="800"] Steel with his family as a kid. Steel's the one rocking the tank top.[/caption]

Steel lives in Madison, Wisconsin with his wife and young son. Musically, the key to Steel's heart is sadness, male falsettos, and primitive instrumentation.
