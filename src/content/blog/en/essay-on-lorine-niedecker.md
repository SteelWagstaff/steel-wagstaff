A few weeks ago, I was invited to write a short essay for the [Edge Effects blog](http://edgeeffects.net). If you're not already familiar with it, Edge Effects is an outstanding blog run by CHE \[the [Center for Culture, History, and Environment](http://nelson.wisc.edu/che/ "Center for Culture, History, and Environment")\], a group that belongs to the University of Wisconsin-Madison’s [Nelson Institute for Environmental Studies](http://nelson.wisc.edu/ "Nelson Institute for Environmental Studies"), which I've been a proud grad student affiliate and member for some time now. In response to their invitation, I wrote a short essay about [Lorine Niedecker](http://edgeeffects.net/lorine-niedecker-ecopoetics), my favorite Wisconsin writer, and ecopoetics, a term of art in my home discipline of English literary studies. You can read the essay [here](http://edgeeffects.net/lorine-niedecker-ecopoetics/). Enjoy!
